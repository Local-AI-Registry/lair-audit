Why now for AMP

The move from WordPress to Sanity is a risk and the best moment to build AI in from day one.

Risk if nothing changes

  • WordPress plugins quietly handled schema, alt text and meta tags. New sites start without them.
  • Heavy, script-built pages are harder for Google and AI crawlers to read in full.
  • Changed page addresses break old links. EsthetixMD already has 532 of 632 inbound links pointing to dead pages.

Opportunity

  • One standard for every new site: schema, llms.txt, crawlable record, redirects
  • One verified record per location that AI reads first, whatever the site is built on
  • Fix name and phone mismatches once, at the source, for all locations

Three situations

Each practice needs something different. That's how a portfolio plan should work too.

Protect the leader

EsthetixMD · Bend, OR

Tied for the top score in Bend (72), with 628 reviews at 4.9. But ChatGPT left it out of 4 of 6 searches and named Elite Medi Spa and Juniper Dermatology instead.

Why: three phone numbers online and the old Exhale Spa name still on its YouTube channel.

Close the gap

Hello Smooth · St Johns, FL

43 against Amason Aesthetics' 74. AI named Amason 20 times to Hello Smooth's 13. No AI tool named it for HydraFacial or microneedling.

Why: name, address or hours differ on every directory we could read. No news coverage at all.

Build depth

Illumé · Lee's Summit, MO

Ahead of its market (57 vs 43), and ChatGPT and Gemini name it every time. But it now ranks for 246 Google searches, down from 597 a year ago.

Why: no videos, no press, 110 reviews against rivals' 300+, and four versions of its name online.

Sources: audit scans Nov 11–12, 2026 (DataForSEO for rankings, links and live Google results; AI answers checked directly).

Rollout scenarios Back pocket

Which practices go first? Four ways to order the rollout.

Triage

Struggling first

Start where the gap is widest, like Hello Smooth (43 vs 74).

  • Biggest visible lift
  • Strong case studies for the rest

Watch: slower start where basics are weak.

Protect

Leaders first

Lock in practices already winning, like EsthetixMD, before rivals catch up.

  • Protects the most revenue
  • Fastest wins

Watch: smaller "before and after" story.

Clusters

Market by market

Do every AMP practice in a region together, sharing local press and guides.

  • Shared content lowers effort
  • Regional exclusivity

Watch: depends on regional density.

Migration-led

Follow the Sanity build

Each practice starts the week its new site goes live, with the record built in.

  • Protects rankings through the move
  • No rework later

Watch: pace set by the build schedule.

A mix is common: a small pilot from two or three of these, then the full rollout.

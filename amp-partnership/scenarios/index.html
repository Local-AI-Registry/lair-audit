Concessions we can trade

Give these in exchange for commitment, not up front.

We can give

  • Free scans of every AMP location (portfolio view) before signing
  • First month at the pilot rate for each new wave
  • Day-one acquisition audits at no charge for Strategic
  • Sanity record standard and redirect map included in Portfolio and up
  • Case-study credit on pricing in exchange for named results

We ask for

  • Minimum location count or a 6–12 month term for portfolio tiers
  • One AMP approver and a 5-business-day approval window
  • Publishing access on the new Sanity sites
  • Permission to name AMP and results in case studies
  • Referrals and introductions (5–15% lifetime referral share available)

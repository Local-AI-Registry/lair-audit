What didn't, and the fix

Straight answers on what has not moved yet, and what we are doing about each.

#9 to #31"naturopath ottawa" slipped

720 searches a month. Several NutriChem pages split this search, so Google is unsure which one to show.

Fix: the topical map gives it one Clinic hub page that owns it, with the others pointing to it.

#2 to #5"compounding pharmacy ottawa" dropped

480 searches a month. Ahrefs has it at #5 and Semrush at #2, so it is still on page one, but Ahrefs shows it lower than in April.

Fix: one Compounding page owns every compounding search. It is drafted and ready to go live.

0 of 12New pages are not live yet

We drafted 12 pages in three weeks. The first four were full pages built in your Elementor theme, not blog posts, and the build took longer than planned. That is on us. Two wait on answers from your team, and the two profiles are on hold while you build your own.

Fix: Blood Work, Retail Store, integrative care and compounding are being built now. We will send you each link the day it goes live.

146 to 147Reviews stalled in October

Pharmacy reviews grew 23 between August and October, then one in the last three weeks. The Clinic sits at 30.

Fix: put the counter placard at both desks and turn on automatic review requests after each visit.

RoboticTone of posts and replies

You and Rebecca were right: some drafts read like a machine, and one Clinic post talked about pharmacy services.

Fix: a human editor now reads every public post and reply, and each location only posts about its own services.

27 to 7Google AI Mode citations fell

In Ahrefs' sample, Google's AI Mode cites NutriChem less than in July, even as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini cite it more.

Fix: the new pages each open with a short, direct answer, the format AI Mode quotes, and the Business Page gives Google's AI verified facts.

June 12Google Analytics has been off since June

So we cannot yet count bookings and calls from search. Search Console is the full record for this report.

Fix: the Analytics events agreed on November 4, plus "How did you find us?" on the booking form.

We need to move faster

These are not blog posts. Each page is built to rank and to be quoted by AI: a short, direct answer up front, a named NutriChem clinician as author or reviewer with a verified profile behind them, structured data that tells Google and the AI tools exactly what the page covers, and links that tie it into the map so one page owns each search and passes its strength to the rest. Each one is built by hand in your Elementor theme. That takes care, and it is why the first batch took longer than planned.

The pages already live prove the approach. The September clean-up lifted daily visits 28%, and nine health articles now rank #1 and feed the AI answers. The approved map has 136 pages. At four a week, the rest takes about seven months. Three ways to speed it up:

0148-hour reviews

Pages publish two business days after they arrive unless you flag a change. Anything can be edited after it is live.

02Explainers follow the voice card

Health explainers publish on the voice card we rebuilt from your own pages. You review clinic service pages and anything with a price or a doctor's name.

03Six pages a week

Same review process with a bigger weekly batch, so the map finishes in about five months.

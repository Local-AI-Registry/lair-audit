"I searched to page 6 and gave up."

Checked live on 2 November from outside Omaha, rmsdumpsters.com is #17 for "dumpster rental omaha ne," near the top of page 2. Google changes results by device, location and past searches, so one person's page 6 can be another's page 2. Page 1 is the goal, and the next 30 days below are aimed at it.

"I see my ad and Places on page 1, but nothing else."

They come from different places. Your ad is paid. Places comes from your Google profile and is sorted largely by distance, so you show near your yard and other companies show in other parts of town. The regular results come from your website, and Google is still re-checking the new site that went live on 13 October.

"Google AI did not show us for concrete washout."

AI answers only name businesses whose pages describe the service. Concrete washout was not one of the focus areas we set together (large contractors, home builders and residential customers), so it is not on your website yet. If you want it to be a focus, tell Caron and we will build the page and point posts at it.

"Other SEO companies are calling me."

That is normal right now. Those companies run tools that flag businesses with a new website or with addresses that do not match across the web, then they cold call. Your old address was listed in many places for years, so a few directories still show it. We are cleaning them up and they correct over time as each site updates.

The October dip

October was a down month for all of Omaha. RMS held up better than most.

Your new website went live on October 13. When a site moves to new pages, Google re-checks each one before it trusts it again, so the number of searches you show up for dipped. Most of those were small searches that send few visits. Your actual visits barely moved, and the numbers have held flat since mid-October. That is what the bottom of a relaunch dip looks like.

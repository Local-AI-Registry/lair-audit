## Questions to ask any provider

1. **Who does the work?** You, inside software, or a team doing it for you?
2. **What do they measure?** Rankings and clicks, or what each AI assistant actually says about you, with screenshots?
3. **What happens off your website?** Our research found businesses' own sites were 6.5% of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions. Ask how they earn the other 93.5%.
4. **Do they check many phrasings?** Only ~35% of cited sources stayed the same when a question was reworded.
5. **What are the terms?** Contract length, and what happens if nothing changes in 90 days.

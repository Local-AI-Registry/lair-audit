## Terms

### 1. Who this is between

This Business Associate Agreement (the “Agreement”) is between the healthcare practice signing below (the “Covered Entity”) and Blind LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company doing business as Local AI Registry (the “Business Associate”). It applies to any Protected Health Information (“PHI”) the Business Associate creates, receives, maintains or transmits for the Covered Entity while providing its services.

### 2. Definitions

Terms used but not defined here, such as Breach, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Protected Health Information, Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information and Use, have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.

### 3. What we do and don't touch

Our services (verified business records, Google Business Profile management, review replies, listings, content and AI visibility work) are designed to work without PHI. We do not need patient records and ask that you not send them. If PHI reaches us anyway, for example in a review, a message or a booking request, this Agreement governs how we handle it.

### 4. Our obligations

The Business Associate agrees to: (a) not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this Agreement or as Required by Law; (b) use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 CFR Part 164 for electronic PHI, to prevent use or disclosure other than as provided here; (c) report to the Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by this Agreement, including Breaches of Unsecured PHI as required by 45 CFR 164.410, and any Security Incident it becomes aware of; (d) ensure that any Subcontractors that create, receive, maintain or transmit PHI on its behalf agree to the same restrictions and conditions, in line with 45 CFR 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2); (e) make PHI in a Designated Record Set available as needed for the Covered Entity to meet 45 CFR 164.524; (f) make amendments to PHI in a Designated Record Set as directed under 45 CFR 164.526; (g) keep and provide the information needed for an accounting of disclosures under 45 CFR 164.528; (h) to the extent it carries out a Covered Entity obligation under Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164, comply with the requirements that apply to the Covered Entity; and (i) make its internal practices, books and records available to the Secretary for determining compliance with the HIPAA Rules.

### 5. Permitted uses and disclosures

The Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as necessary to perform its services for the Covered Entity, as Required by Law, and for its proper management and administration or to carry out its legal responsibilities, as permitted by 45 CFR 164.504(e)(4). It will follow the Minimum Necessary standard and will not use or disclose PHI in a way that would violate Subpart E of 45 CFR Part 164 if done by the Covered Entity. Review replies are written so they never confirm that a reviewer is a patient.

### 6. Your obligations

The Covered Entity will tell the Business Associate about any limitation in its notice of privacy practices, any change in or revocation of an Individual's permission, and any restriction it has agreed to, to the extent it affects our use or disclosure of PHI. It will not ask us to use or disclose PHI in any way not permitted under the HIPAA Rules if done by the Covered Entity.

### 7. Term and termination

This Agreement starts on the date it is signed and ends when the services end or when either party terminates it. If either party determines the other has violated a material term, it may terminate this Agreement and the related services if the violation is not cured within 30 days. When it ends, the Business Associate will return or destroy all PHI it still holds, or, if that is not feasible, extend these protections to that PHI and limit further use and disclosure for as long as it keeps it.

### 8. General

References to the HIPAA Rules mean the sections as in effect or as amended. The parties will amend this Agreement as needed to comply with the law. Any ambiguity will be read to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, except where federal law controls.

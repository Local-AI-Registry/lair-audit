## How many businesses AI names

**73%**

73% of the 128,400+ local businesses Local AI Registry has scored are named by zero AI assistants (our estimate from AI-visibility checks).

Source: [Local AI Registry data](../methodology/index.html)

**85.6%**

In a census of every venue in two markets, 85.6% were never recommended by any of four assistants. Being named was associated with having a website, more reviews, listed prices and web mentions (Pitenin 2026, preprint, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**3.5%**

Only 3.5% of businesses ChatGPT named in localized searches appeared at every test point across a town (BrightLocal, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**1 in 75**

Across the businesses we've scored, nearly two in three land below 45 out of 100 on the AI Score, and barely one in seventy-five clears 65.

Source: [What is an AI Score?](../journal/what-is-an-ai-score/index.html)

## Where AI gets its answers

**76.4%**

Local business searches triggered a Google AI Overview 76.4% of the time, compared with 13.7% of all searches.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**67.5%**

News outlets supplied 67.5% of the citations in Google's AI answers about local businesses.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**6.5%**

Businesses' own websites supplied just 6.5% of those citations.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**70 of 147**

Brand websites ranked on page one for 147 of 157 brand questions, but AI cited them in only 70.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**0.737**

In a 75,000-brand study, the top correlate of AI visibility was YouTube and branded web mentions (0.737), not links or pages (Ahrefs, cited in our white paper The Convergence).

Source: [The Convergence](../proof/the-convergence-white-paper/index.html)

**51.6%**

For claims AI attributed to a store locator, app or official site, 51.6% were not supported by any page it cited.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**78%**

Cited social posts had a median of 8 readable words, and 78% were too thin to verify anything.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

## How AI answers change

**29%**

When ChatGPT got the same local question about 39 minutes apart, a median of 29% of the businesses it named appeared in both answers (8ight.AI, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**0.46**

Asked the same query twice, Google Search shared 0.78 of its sources, AI Overviews 0.66 and Gemini 0.46 (Grossman et al., SIGIR 2026, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**two-thirds**

Rewording the same local question changed about two-thirds of the sources Google's AI cited.

Source: [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-001

**100 answers**

For a business named in 40% of answers, the likely range is roughly 17% to 69% after 10 answers, and 31% to 50% after 100 (our calculation).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

## Local searches vs question searches

## Healthcare and specialists

**74.7%**

Asking for "an oculoplastic surgeon" returned specialists 74.7% of the time, compared with 46.6% for "a doctor who does eyelid lifts" (Parikh et al., 2024, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**63.9%**

Doctors named by AI matched real clinicians 63.9% of the time with web search, compared with 10.8% without it (Ibrahim and Zaki, 2026, preprint, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

**52.8%**

ChatGPT declined to recommend an orthopedic surgeon in 52.8% of 40,500 persona-based requests (Haupt et al., 2026, reviewed in our brief).

Source: [How Often AI Recommends Your Business](../research/how-often-ai-recommends-your-business/index.html) · LAIR-R-2026-002

## What does the research say?

From [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our study of how Google's AI answers local business questions. Every number links to its chart.

**Local business searches trigger Google AI Overviews more than any other topic.** 76.4% of local-business searches showed a Google AI Overview, nearly double the next category (science, 39.9%) and over five times the 13.7% average.Figure 3, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category.png)

**Who Google’s AI cites for local business questions vs everything else.** In local answers, Gannett newspapers supplied 28.3% of citations and appeared in 90.3% of answers. Businesses’ own websites supplied 6.5% of citations.Figure 5, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap.png)

**Ranked on page one, skipped by the AI: brand websites in local answers.** Brand websites appeared on page one in 147 of 157 brand questions but were cited in 70. Walmart ranked in 11 of 15 answers and was cited once.Figure 10, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#brand-website-ranked-vs-cited-google-ai-overviews), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/brand-website-ranked-vs-cited-google-ai-overviews.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/brand-website-ranked-vs-cited-google-ai-overviews.png)

**173 of 207 local AI answers told people to check with their local store.** 83.6% of local AI answers sent the reader back to the store (“check your local store,” “call ahead,” “hours may vary”), against 2.6% of other answers.Figure 14, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-answers-telling-people-to-check-local-store-waffle), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-answers-telling-people-to-check-local-store-waffle.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-answers-telling-people-to-check-local-store-waffle.png)

LUXURGERY: Google visits more than doubled

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry
Updated November 16, 2026
SSLUXURGERYDr. Sachin Shridharani · Plastic surgery · New York, NY

Dr. Sachin Shridharani's New York practice has worked with our team on search for about three years. In June 2026 we began working on how AI sees him. Two months later his Google traffic jumped, and ChatGPT now names him among its top three for his signature surgery.

2,364estimated Google visits a month in Nov 2026, up from 1,025 in Jun 2026
4 of 4ChatGPT runs on Nov 15, 2026 put him in the top three for “which plastic surgeon in New York does a drainless tummy tuck”
26Google searches where he ranks #1, up from 19 in Jun 2026

Before and after

Through 2025 the practice's Google traffic drifted down, from about 1,070 estimated visits a month in January to about 600 by October.

What we did: the 11 signals

Every client gets the same program. We work on the 11 things AI checks before it names a local business, and we measure what each assistant says every month.

Top 3 in 4 of 4 runs for “Drainless tummy tuck, New York.” The middle column shows pages and facts ChatGPT and Perplexity cited when asked about this client.

What AI says now

We asked ChatGPT the kind of specific question a patient asks when they already know what they want. We asked it four times on Nov 15, 2026. It named him every time: once first, twice second and once third. In each run it knew his signature drainless technique, TubelessTuck, by name. The screenshots below are two of those runs, taken in ChatGPT that afternoon.

Asked about the practice by name, Perplexity got the facts right: board certified, Johns Hopkins trained, 880 Fifth Avenue, TubelessTuck and a 4.87 Zocdoc rating. It cited his own site, AEDIT, Haute Living, WebMD and Zocdoc.

What we're working on next

The next step is the broad questions, like “best rhinoplasty surgeon in New York.” More outside coverage and reviews that name his specialties give AI more to quote there.

How we measured it

Google visits and #1 rankings are DataForSEO estimates for Google US, pulled Nov 15, 2026. Screenshots were taken in ChatGPT the same afternoon, each in a new temporary chat with no memory or personalization. AI answers change from day to day, so every result here carries the date it was captured. AI is evolving every day, and our dedicated local AI research team is always working out the best approach.

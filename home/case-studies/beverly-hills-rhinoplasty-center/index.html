Dr. Dugar: ChatGPT's first pick for closed rhinoplasty

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 16, 2026
DDBeverly Hills Rhinoplasty CenterDr. Deepak Dugar · Facial plastic surgery · Beverly Hills, CA

Dr. Deepak Dugar runs Beverly Hills Rhinoplasty Center, known for scarless, closed rhinoplasty. His web studio runs his website. We run his Google profile and how AI sees him.

3 of 3ChatGPT runs named him for “best closed rhinoplasty surgeon in Los Angeles,” Nov 13, 2026
#1in the first run, called “My first consultation pick”
45ChatGPT questions that pulled up his site by Nov 2026, up from 0 before we started in Aug 2025

Before and after

Before we started with Dr. Dugar in August 2025, his site came up in 0 ChatGPT questions in our tracking. Two months later it came up in 5. The running total has grown every quarter since, to 45.

What we did: the 11 signals

Every client gets the same program. We work on the 11 things AI checks before it names a local business, and we measure what each assistant says every month.

First consultation pick for “Closed rhinoplasty, Los Angeles.” The middle column shows pages and facts ChatGPT and Perplexity cited when asked about this client.

What AI says now

We asked ChatGPT the same question three times in a row. It named Dr. Dugar every time, first in one run and second in the other two. In the first run it labeled him “My first consultation pick.” Since June 2026 ChatGPT has also named him for “best rhinoplasty surgeon in Beverly Hills” and “best nose surgeon in the United States.”

Asked about him by name, Perplexity called him a highly specialized, double board certified closed rhinoplasty surgeon, citing the LA Times, his own site, RealSelf, the Rhinoplasty Society and WebMD.

What we're working on next

He's already named in 2 of 3 runs for the broadest question, “best rhinoplasty surgeon in Los Angeles.” Moving him to the top of that list is the next step.

How we measured it

ChatGPT questions come from DataForSEO's LLM mentions database. Screenshots are from our AI Answer Watch runs on Nov 13, 2026. AI answers change from day to day, so every result here carries the date it was captured. AI is evolving every day, and our dedicated local AI research team is always working out the best approach.

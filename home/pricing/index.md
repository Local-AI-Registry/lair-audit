## What the work includes, in detail

These are the jobs that run on your account every month in the program, described the way they actually run:

- **[AI monitoring](../signals/ai-monitoring/index.html).** Once a month we ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask, and we keep the answer each one gave.
- **[Google profile posts](../signals/google-profile/index.html).** We write the posts on your Google Business Profile and send each one to you before it publishes.
- **[Review replies](../signals/reputation/index.html).** The review pass runs every three hours against your Google listing. We draft a reply to each new three, four and five star review on your Google listing, and you edit, approve or decline it.
- **[Directory listings](../signals/directory-listings/index.html).** Twenty three general hosts for everyone: Yelp, Apple Maps, Facebook, the BBB, Yellow Pages, Foursquare, Nextdoor, Trustpilot and the rest. Your trade adds its own on top, which takes a medical practice to forty: Zocdoc, Healthgrades, WebMD, RealSelf, Vitals and eleven more.
- **[Photos](../signals/photo-codifying/index.html).** We collect your photos, write the words that travel with each one, and publish them onto your Google listing a few at a time.
- **[Press releases](../signals/pr/index.html).** You choose what the news is, we draft the release, and nothing goes to the wire until you have signed it off.
- **[The monthly report](../tour/index.html).** Every thirty days you get a PDF of what was done on your account and what moved, counted from the day you bought rather than from the first of the month.

Plus SEO, website, video, outside articles and best-of guides. [See all 11](../signals/index.html).

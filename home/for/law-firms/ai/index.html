Short answerAI assistants recommend the law firms they can confirm across many trusted sources. For law firms, the biggest levers are articles on other sites, a clear, well-coded website and reviews, working together with the other signals.

Why AI matters for law firms

Legal clients need someone they can trust in a stressful moment, and many now ask AI to shortlist attorneys. Assistants look for clear practice areas, credentials, reviews and independent recognition.

The signals that matter most for law firms

01
Outside articles
Legal directories, bar association profiles and recognitions confirm standing.
02
Website
A page per practice area and attorney, with Attorney and LegalService schema.
03
Reviews
Reviews across Google and legal directories describe outcomes and service.
04
Press coverage
Expert quotes in local news build authority in a competitive field.

The other seven of the 11 signals still count. These four are where most law firms have the biggest gaps.

Rules law firms need to follow

Follow your state bar's advertising rules, including rules on testimonials, results and the word "specialist". Never reveal client details in review replies.

Frequently asked questions

Do legal directories matter for AI?

Yes. They are common sources for legal questions, so keep your profiles complete and consistent.

Should each attorney have a page?

Yes. Clients search by name, and individual pages connect each attorney to the firm.

How long does it take for a law firm to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our 90-day guarantee reflects that timeline.

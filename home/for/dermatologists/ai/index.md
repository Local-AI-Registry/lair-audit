## Why AI matters for dermatologists

Dermatology mixes medical and cosmetic care, and patients ask AI about both: insurance, wait times, specific conditions and treatments. Practices that state those details clearly are the ones assistants can recommend with confidence.

## Rules dermatologists need to follow

List providers with their credentials and specialties. Keep insurer directories current, since wrong insurance information is a common source of conflicting data. Follow patient privacy rules in every review reply.

## Frequently asked questions

Should I list the insurance plans I take?

Yes. It's one of the most common questions, and an accurate list lets AI answer it.

How do I show both medical and cosmetic expertise?

Give each its own section and pages. Mixed pages make it harder for AI to recommend you for either.

How long does it take for a dermatology practice to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our [90-day guarantee](../../../guarantee/index.html) reflects that timeline.

## What does the research say?

From [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our study of how Google's AI answers local business questions. Every number links to its chart.

[**76.4%** of local business searches showed a Google AI answer, more than any other topic](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category)[**1.0%** of citations went to the business itself for generic “what's open” questions](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-citation-sources-by-local-question-type-heatmap)[**6.5%** of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions were the businesses' own websites](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap)

**Local business searches trigger Google AI Overviews more than any other topic.** 76.4% of local-business searches showed a Google AI Overview, nearly double the next category (science, 39.9%) and over five times the 13.7% average.Figure 3, [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category.svg) · [PNG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category.png)

**The vaguer the local question, the less the business gets cited.** Businesses’ own sites made up 8.8 to 10.9% of citations when a brand was named, but only 1.0% for generic “what’s open” questions, which is how people discover new places.Figure 9, [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-citation-sources-by-local-question-type-heatmap), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-citation-sources-by-local-question-type-heatmap.svg) · [PNG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-citation-sources-by-local-question-type-heatmap.png)

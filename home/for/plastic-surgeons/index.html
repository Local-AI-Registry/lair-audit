What patients ask AI before they book a consult

Surgery is a big decision, so patients ask AI question after question. Every answer is built from what AI can verify about you.

  • “Who is the best rhinoplasty surgeon near me?”

    AI needs: Your procedures named clearly on your site and profiles, with reviews that mention them.

  • “Is this surgeon board certified?”

    AI needs: Your board certification stated the same way on every source AI checks.

  • “How much does a tummy tuck cost here?”

    AI needs: Price ranges or financing details AI can quote instead of guessing.

  • “Which surgeon has the best facelift results?”

    AI needs: Before-and-after photos labeled so AI knows which procedure each one shows.

  • “Who specializes in revision rhinoplasty?”

    AI needs: Pages and articles that show depth in a procedure, not just a list of services.

  • “What is recovery like after breast augmentation?”

    AI needs: Plain-English answers on your own site, with your name on them.

We build and maintain those answers for you, so AI has a reason to say your name.

Questions surgeons ask us

How does ChatGPT decide which plastic surgeons to recommend?

It recommends surgeons it can verify. That means the same facts on Google, directories and review sites, a board certification it can confirm, procedures it can match to the question, and recent reviews. When those sources disagree or say little, it leaves you out.

Is this different from regular SEO?

Yes. SEO gets your website ranked in a list of links. AI search reads many sources at once and names only a few surgeons. You need both. We focus on the part most agencies miss: what AI reads about you away from your own website.

Will this work with my current marketing agency?

Yes. We do not replace your website, ads or social media. We handle the listings, reviews, credentials and monthly AI checks that agencies usually leave alone, and we are happy to share our reports with your agency.

How do you keep review replies HIPAA-safe?

Our replies never confirm that someone is a patient and never mention a procedure or any health details. Every reply lands in your inbox before it goes out, and you have three business days to change it or stop it.

Which AI tools do you track?

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Claude and Perplexity. Your monthly report shows what each one says about your practice and what changed.

How long until AI starts naming my practice?

Our goal is about 3 months, and some practices see changes sooner. If AI is not naming you in 90 days, we keep working for free until it does.

## Why AI matters for plastic surgeons

Patients choosing a surgeon research for weeks and increasingly ask AI to compare credentials, results and reviews. An assistant that can't confirm board certification or find independent coverage will name someone else.

## Rules plastic surgeons need to follow

State board certification exactly as the certifying board names it, and link to the board's lookup where possible. Follow advertising rules for medical professionals in your state and keep patient privacy rules in mind in every review reply. Never confirm in public that someone is a patient.

## Frequently asked questions

Does a surgeon's personal profile matter?

Yes. Patients often ask about the surgeon by name. A strong individual profile, linked to the practice, helps AI connect the two.

Can I reply to reviews without breaking privacy rules?

Yes, with general replies that don't confirm treatment or share details. When in doubt, take it offline.

How long does it take for a plastic surgery practice to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our [90-day guarantee](../../../guarantee/index.html) reflects that timeline.

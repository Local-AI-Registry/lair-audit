How to get your plumbing company recommended by ChatGPT, Google and Gemini

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 13, 2026
Short answerAI assistants recommend the plumbers they can confirm across many trusted sources. For plumbers, the biggest levers are a complete Google profile, reviews and consistent directory listings, working together with the other signals.

Why AI matters for plumbers

Plumbing problems rarely wait, and customers ask assistants for someone who can come now and won't overcharge. Assistants name a small number of businesses, so being one of them matters more than ranking tenth on a page of results.

The signals that matter most for plumbers

01
Google profile
Emergency hours, service area and services listed answer the urgent question.
02
Reviews
Reviews about response time and fair pricing are what customers and AI look for.
03
Directory listings
Home service platforms and your main listings must match.
04
Website

The other seven of the 11 signals still count. These four are where most plumbers have the biggest gaps.

Rules plumbers need to follow

Show your license and insurance. Be clear about trip fees, emergency rates and service areas.

Frequently asked questions

Does listing prices help?

Price questions are common. Starting prices or how you price jobs help assistants answer them.

Should I answer the phone 24/7?

If you advertise emergency service, yes. Reviews about missed calls quickly undo the benefit.

How long does it take for a plumbing company to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our 90-day guarantee reflects that timeline.

Why AI matters for roofers

A new roof is one of the biggest bills a homeowner faces, and storm season brings a rush of new companies. Homeowners ask AI who is licensed, established and trustworthy, which favors businesses with a long, consistent record online.

Frequently asked questions

Do job photos help with AI?

Yes, when they're labeled with the roof type, material and area. They show real work AI can describe.

How do I compete with storm chasers?

Time in business, local address, license details and a long review history are hard to fake. Make them easy to find.

How long does it take for a roofing company to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our 90-day guarantee reflects that timeline.

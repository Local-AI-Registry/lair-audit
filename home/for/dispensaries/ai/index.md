## Why AI matters for dispensaries

Cannabis businesses face limits on advertising across many platforms, which makes organic visibility, including AI answers, especially valuable. Customers ask assistants who is open, who has deals and who has the best selection nearby.

## Rules dispensaries need to follow

Follow your state's cannabis advertising rules on every page and listing, including age gating and health claims. Some platforms limit cannabis content, so keep the facts accurate on the ones you can use and on your own site.

## Frequently asked questions

Can AI assistants recommend dispensaries?

Many assistants answer factual questions about where licensed dispensaries are and when they're open. Accurate data is what they draw on.

Do menus matter for AI?

An up-to-date menu on your site and the major cannabis platforms helps answer questions about products and deals.

How long does it take for a dispensary to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our [90-day guarantee](../../../guarantee/index.html) reflects that timeline.

Urgent care

AI visibility for urgent care centers

Urgent care is the one trade here where the person asking needs an answer in the next hour, and the questions show it. Hours, insurance, wait time, walk-ins.

The goal: When someone asks ChatGPT for urgent care in Tampa, you are in the answer.

What urgent care centers are up against

The two biggest questions we hold in this trade are hours and insurance, in that order. Neither is about how good you are.

The rest are operational: parking, wait times, whether you handle a fracture, whether you see children, what to bring. An assistant can only answer those off a record that says so.

New data from our research team
76.4%

Local business searches triggered a Google AI Overview 76.4% of the time, compared with 13.7% of all searches. The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001

85.6%

In a census of every venue in two markets, 85.6% were never recommended by any of four assistants. Being named was associated with having a website, more reviews, listed prices and web mentions (Pitenin 2026, preprint, reviewed in our brief). How Often AI Recommends Your Business, LAIR-R-2026-002

What we do for urgent care centers

The work that matters most here, and the feature page that explains each one in full.

Directory listings

Hours and address pushed out as one record, because an answer about who is open now is assembled from whichever source the assistant reached.

Posts

Posts that answer the operational questions: walk-ins, what to bring, what you treat and what you send on.

Review replies

A wait-time complaint answered well is worth more here than anywhere else, because wait time is the thing people are reading reviews for.

Frequently asked questions

Our hours change with staffing. Can you keep up?

Your listing is yours to change and the change pushes out from one record. What we control is that every place we sent your hours agrees with the place you changed them.

Can you publish our wait time?

Only if you give us a figure and it is one you stand behind. We do not state a number nobody measured.

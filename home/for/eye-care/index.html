What eye care practices are up against

Optometrists, ophthalmologists and lasik surgeons are one market to the person asking. An assistant answering "eye doctor near me" does not sort by degree, so a practice competing only against its own profession is competing against a third of the field.

Insurance is the second question, every time. A listing that does not say which plans it takes loses the answer to one that does.

What our research says AI actually reads

Before you compare tools, it helps to know where AI answers come from. Our research team pulled 7,583 of Google's AI answers and read every source they cited. The pattern is clear: AI builds a local answer mostly from what other sites say about you, not from your own website or your listings alone.

6.5%of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions were the businesses' own websites
67.5%of citations in local AI answers came from news coverage
1 of 207local AI answers gave a street address or phone number
~35%of cited sources stayed the same when the same question was worded differently

Source: The Local Answer Gap, Local AI Registry research, 2026.

Two things follow. First, consistent listings are the floor, not the ceiling: almost no answer repeats your address or phone, so a listings tool alone can't put you in the answer. Second, the sources shift when a question is worded differently, so you need to watch many phrasings every month, not one keyword. In our own AI Answer Watch screenshots, 27 of 30 ChatGPT answers we screenshotted listed RealSelf among their sources, which is why industry review sites matter so much in medical aesthetics.

The directories that matter for eye care practices

Every business gets checked on 23 general directories: Yelp, Apple Maps, Facebook, the BBB, Yellow Pages, Foursquare, Nextdoor, Trustpilot and the rest. Because AI reads you as a medical practice, your roster grows to 40, adding Zocdoc, Healthgrades, WebMD, RealSelf, Vitals and more. A directory where we can't find you is recorded as not found, which is a different fact from not listed, and every reading is retired after 30 days so you never see a stale number.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for optometrists as well as ophthalmologists?

Yes. The registry holds both, and the searches that matter do not separate them. The work is the same either way.

Can you say which insurance we take?

Only where you have told us. Insurance is a fact about your practice, so it comes from you and goes onto your listing and your directory records.

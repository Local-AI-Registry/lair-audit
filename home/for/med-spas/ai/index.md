## Why AI matters for med spas

Med spa clients research hard before they book, because treatments touch their face and their budget. Many now ask an assistant to shortlist providers, compare prices and check safety. The assistant names a few businesses, and most clients never look past them.

## Rules med spas need to follow

Name your medical director and licensed injectors with their credentials; trust signals matter more in medical aesthetics than almost anywhere. Follow your state's rules on who can perform each treatment, and get written consent before posting any before and after photo. Use brand names like Botox correctly and avoid promising results.

## Frequently asked questions

Should I list prices on my website?

Where you can, yes. Price is one of the most common questions clients ask AI. A clear starting price or range helps assistants answer with your business in mind.

Do before and after photos help with AI?

They help clients more than machines. Label them clearly, follow consent rules and place them on the page for that treatment.

How long does it take for a med spa to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our [90-day guarantee](../../../guarantee/index.html) reflects that timeline.

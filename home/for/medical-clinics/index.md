## Frequently asked questions

We are a clinic, not urgent care. Does that matter?

It matters for what we write. The demand is worded as urgent care and your listing should say plainly what you do and do not do, rather than borrowing a word that would misdescribe you.

How quickly do hours changes reach the assistants?

Your listing updates immediately. What an assistant says catches up on its own schedule, which is why we re-ask your questions every month and show you the answer.

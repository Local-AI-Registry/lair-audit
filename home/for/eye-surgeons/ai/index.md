## Why AI matters for eye surgeons

Eye surgery is a high-trust decision, and patients research technology, surgeon experience and price. Assistants are increasingly the first stop, and they lean on sources that explain procedures clearly and confirm the surgeon's credentials.

## Rules eye surgeons need to follow

State surgeon credentials and fellowship training accurately. Describe outcomes carefully, following advertising rules for medical procedures, and avoid guarantees about vision results. Respect patient privacy in every testimonial.

## Frequently asked questions

Should I explain who is not a candidate?

Yes. Honest candidacy information builds trust with patients and gives AI balanced content to share.

Do technology names matter?

Yes. Patients ask about specific procedures and lasers by name. Name the ones you use on the matching pages.

How long does it take for a eye surgery practice to show up in AI?

It depends on where you start and how competitive your area is. Fixes to your facts and profile can show up within weeks; reviews, lists and articles build over months. Our [90-day guarantee](../../../guarantee/index.html) reflects that timeline.

## Frequently asked questions

Our treatments are on our website already. Why is that not enough?

Retrieval happens at passage level. A services page that lists names without describing them has nothing an answer can quote, which is a different problem from not existing.

Can you write about products we sell?

We can name a product you stock. We cannot say what it will do to somebody, which is where most product copy in this trade goes wrong.

**Short answer** Your site needs to load fast, work on a phone, state plainly what you do and where, and include structured data (schema) that labels your business, services, hours and reviews in a format machines read without guessing.

## What AI needs from your site

Assistants and search crawlers read your site's code, not its design. They need clear facts: who you are, what services you offer, where, when you're open and how to book. Structured data, often called [schema markup](../../glossary/index.html#schema-markup), states those facts in a standard format so there's nothing to guess.

## The checklist

- Pages load quickly on a phone.
- Every service has its own page with a plain description.
- LocalBusiness schema with name, address, phone, hours and geo-coordinates.
- Service and FAQ schema where they fit.
- No important text hidden inside images or behind clicks.
- An [llms.txt](../../glossary/index.html#llms-txt) file that points AI to your key pages (optional, low effort).

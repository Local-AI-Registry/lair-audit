**Short answer** Reviews are one of the main things assistants use to decide who to recommend. They look at your rating, how many reviews you have, how recent they are, what customers say in their own words, and whether you reply. Reviews on several sites count more than reviews on one.

## What AI reads in your reviews

An assistant doesn't just see a star rating. It reads review text to learn what you're known for. If customers often mention a service, a staff member or a quality like "on time" or "explained everything", the assistant can repeat that when someone asks for exactly that.

## What we do for you

We draft a reply to each new three, four and five star review on your Google listing, and you edit, approve or decline it. A reply to a one or two star review is read by our team first, and nothing goes up under your name unless you approve it.

A reply answers the person, not the room. The first sentence responds to what this reviewer said and names what the visit was for. It adds one thing a stranger reading before booking would want, and stops.

A one or two star review is never answered without your approval, at any age. It is flagged to you on your reviews page with what it says, whether it breaks a Google policy and where the removal petition stands. One that lands while you are with us also emails you within hours; one already sitting on the listing when you arrived is flagged without the mail, because a notice about a review from 2019 reads as news and is not.

Thin replies already on your listing are rewritten, up to twenty of them, and never the one under a one or two star review. Four words of thanks under a review that named a treatment is a wasted paragraph on a page an assistant reads.

### What you get

- A drafted reply per unanswered three, four or five star review, newest first.
- A flag on your reviews page for each low-star review, and an email within hours for one less than a fortnight old, with the petition state stated plainly and never overstated.
- The three steps to report a review from your own Google account, because some of them are yours to file and not ours.
- Replies published on your listing under your name.

### How it runs

1. **Reviews are read** The review pass runs every three hours against your Google listing.
2. **Three stars and up are drafted** At most twenty drafts in a run, and at most five a day for reviews older than six months, so a back catalogue is worked through rather than dumped.
3. **One and two stars are pursued** Each one from the last six months is read against Google policy and gets a petition written and assigned to one of our people, because Google only accepts these from a person, so one of our team submits it by hand.
4. **You approve or leave it** Replies to three, four and five star reviews are in the default set that publishes after three business days if untouched. Switch the type to manual and nothing moves without you.

**Why it moves the AI answer** Reviews are the text an assistant quotes when it explains why it picked somebody, and a reply is the only part of that text you get to write.

## What helps most

- A steady flow of new reviews, not a burst once a year.
- Reviews on more than one site: Google plus the review sites your industry uses.
- Customers naming the service they came for.
- A short, personal reply to every review, good or bad.

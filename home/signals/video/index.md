**Short answer** Video helps in two ways. Assistants that search the web can read video titles, descriptions and transcripts, and YouTube results often appear in Google's AI answers. A clear video that answers a real customer question gives AI one more trustworthy source about you.

## What AI reads from a video

Most assistants don't watch your video. They read its title, description, captions and the page it sits on. A video titled "Lobby tour" says nothing. A video titled with the exact question a customer asks, with accurate captions, answers it.

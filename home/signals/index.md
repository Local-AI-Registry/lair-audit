# The 11 signals AI checks before it recommends a local business

> The eleven signals ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI use to decide which local business to recommend, with a guide to checking and fixing each one.

Source: https://localairegistry.com/signals/
Updated: 2026-11-15
Publisher: Local AI Registry (https://localairegistry.com)

The 11 signals

By [Steve Lee](../team/steve-lee/index.html), founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 15, 2026

**Short answer** AI assistants recommend the local businesses they can confirm from many trusted sources. Those sources fall into eleven signals: listings, press, video, SEO, website, reviews, Google profile, photos, outside articles, best-of lists and monitoring.

When someone asks an AI assistant for a local business, the assistant has to decide which few to name. It decides by checking what it can confirm about each business across the web. We group what it checks into eleven signals. Each one below has its own guide: what it is, how to check yours and how to fix it.

01

**[Directory listings](../signals/directory-listings/index.html)**  
Same name, address, phone and hours everywhere. AI assistants compare what different websites say about your business.

02

**[Press coverage](../signals/pr/index.html)**  
Real news coverage AI can cite. Yes.

03

**[Video](../signals/video/index.html)**  
Explainer videos that answer what customers ask. Video helps in two ways.

04

**[SEO](../signals/seo/index.html)**  
The Google searches you rank for, and should. Yes.

05

**[Website](../signals/web-management/index.html)**  
A fast site with the code AI reads. Your site needs to load fast, work on a phone, state plainly what you do and where, and include structured data (schema) that labels your business, services, hours and reviews in a format machines read without guessing..

06

**[Reviews](../signals/reputation/index.html)**  
Reviews, ratings and replies across sites. Reviews are one of the main things assistants use to decide who to recommend.

07

**[Google profile](../signals/google-profile/index.html)**  
Complete, active and posting every week. Yes, heavily for Google's own AI answers and Gemini, which draw on Google's local data, and indirectly for other assistants that read Google results.

08

**[Photos](../signals/photo-codifying/index.html)**  
Photos labeled so AI can read what they show. Partly.

09

**[Outside articles](../signals/third-party-articles/index.html)**  
Features about you on independent sites. Assistants trust what independent sites say about you more than what you say about yourself.

10

**[Best-of guides](../signals/best-of-guides/index.html)**  
A place on the local best-of lists AI quotes. Often, yes.

11

**[AI monitoring](../signals/ai-monitoring/index.html)**  
What every assistant says about you, each month. Ask it, regularly and the same way each time.

## How the signals add up

No single signal decides the answer. Assistants look for agreement: the same facts in many places, and outside sources that back up what you say about yourself. That's why the [AI Score](../glossary/index.html#ai-score) looks at all eleven together, and why our [methodology](../methodology/index.html) weighs them as a set.

**Short answer** Yes. Many assistants search the web before they answer, and they lean on the pages that already rank well. Google's own AI answers are built on Google search results. Strong SEO is often how a page becomes a source for AI in the first place.

## How SEO and AI connect

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity a local question, the assistant often runs a web search behind the scenes, reads the top results and writes an answer from them. If your pages don't show up in those searches, the assistant may never read them. That is why [AI visibility and SEO](../../signals/seo/index.html) overlap so much: SEO gets you into the reading list, AI visibility gets you into the answer.

Ranking is not the same as being recommended. An assistant can read your top-ranking page and still name a competitor with better [reviews](../../signals/reputation/index.html) or more [outside mentions](../../signals/third-party-articles/index.html). SEO is one signal of eleven.

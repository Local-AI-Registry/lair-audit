# Why do articles on other websites matter for AI visibility?

> Assistants trust what independent sites say about you more than what you say about yourself.

**Short answer** Assistants trust what independent sites say about you more than what you say about yourself. Articles, interviews, guides and partner pages that name your business give AI outside confirmation of who you are and what you're good at.

## Outside proof

When an assistant builds an answer, it pulls from several sources and favors ones that agree. If a local magazine, a partner's blog and an industry guide all describe you the same way, that description becomes the one AI repeats.

New data from our research team

**0.737**

In a 75,000-brand study, the top correlate of AI visibility was YouTube and branded web mentions (0.737), not links or pages (Ahrefs, cited in our white paper The Convergence). [The Convergence](../../proof/the-convergence-white-paper/index.html)

**67.5%**

News outlets supplied 67.5% of the citations in Google's AI answers about local businesses. [The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html)

**6.5%**

Businesses' own websites supplied just 6.5% of those citations. [The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html)

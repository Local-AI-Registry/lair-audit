**Short answer** Yes, heavily for Google's own AI answers and Gemini, which draw on Google's local data, and indirectly for other assistants that read Google results. A complete, accurate and active profile is one of the highest-value fixes for any local business.

## Why it matters beyond Google Maps

Your Google Business Profile is the most widely read record of your business on the internet. [Google's AI answers](../../ai/google-ai-overviews/index.html) and [Gemini](../../ai/gemini/index.html) lean on it directly. Other assistants often see it through search results. When it's thin or out of date, every assistant gets a weaker picture of you.

## What we do for you

We write the posts on your Google Business Profile and send each one to you before it publishes.

A post is drafted from things we hold about you: the services on your listing, the searches you picked, your photos and your brand rules. Nothing is written from a template.

Every draft is checked against our writing standard, then by an automatic check that blocks the usual mistakes: the wrong town, a made-up statistic or a medical claim.

You choose the cadence. Daily, a few times a week, weekly, or paused.

### What you get

- A draft in your approvals queue with the question it is aimed at named on it.
- An email with the draft and the date it publishes if you leave it alone.
- Edit, approve or decline. A declined draft is not published and the next one takes a different angle.
- The published post on your listing, with the date and the text kept on your account.

### How it runs

1. **A slot comes due** Your cadence decides when. A post drafted inside the same window is not drafted again, so a weekly cadence is a post a week and not a post a day.
2. **The draft is written and checked** Written from your own facts, then checked and rewritten up to three times until it passes. A draft that still fails on the third pass never reaches your queue, and the next slot tries a different angle.
3. **You see it first** The draft lands in your queue and an email goes to you with a publish date three business days out. If it sits unread, one reminder follows.
4. **It publishes** On approval, or on the date in its email if you leave it alone. Decline it and it doesn't go up. You can set posts to wait for your yes in your settings.

**Why it moves the AI answer** An assistant answering a local question reads the Google listing, and a listing that has said nothing in four months reads as a business that may not be trading.

**Short answer** AI assistants compare what different websites say about your business. When your name, address, phone number and hours match on every directory, the assistant can be confident it has the right business. When they conflict, it hedges or names someone else.

## What AI is checking

Before an assistant recommends a local business, it has to be sure the business is real, open and where it says it is. It checks that by reading many sources at once: maps apps, review sites, industry directories, data aggregators and your own website. Each one is a vote. Matching votes build confidence; mismatched votes create doubt.

The most common mismatches are small: an old suite number, a former phone number, holiday hours that never got changed back, or a business name written three different ways.

**Why it moves the AI answer** An assistant does not read one source. It reads many, and a business whose address disagrees across them is a business it has reason to leave out.

## How to check yours

1. Search your business name plus your city in Google, Bing and Apple Maps.
2. Open the first two pages of results and write down the name, address, phone and hours each one shows.
3. Mark every line that differs from your own website.
4. Ask ChatGPT and Gemini for your phone number and hours. If they get it wrong, a directory is feeding them the wrong data.

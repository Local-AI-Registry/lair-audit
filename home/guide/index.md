## How AI decides which local business to recommend

When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best dentist near me?" or asks Google's AI for a plumber who can come today, the assistant doesn't hold a ranked list of every business. It looks the question up, reads what it can find, and names the few businesses it is most confident about.

Confidence comes from agreement. If your website, your Google profile, three review sites, a local news story and a best-of list all describe the same business at the same address doing the same thing, the assistant can name you without worrying it's wrong. If your details conflict, your reviews are thin and nobody else mentions you, it plays it safe and names someone else.

So the job isn't to trick an algorithm. It's to make your business the easiest one in your area to confirm.

## Start here: the three steps ChatGPT itself gives

On Nov 13, 2026 we asked ChatGPT "how do I get my local business recommended by ChatGPT?" Its first steps weren't about content or links. They were about making sure it can find and read your business at all. Do these first; each takes minutes.

1. **Let ChatGPT read your website** ChatGPT finds websites with OpenAI's search crawler, called OAI-SearchBot. If your site blocks it, ChatGPT can't cite your pages. Ask whoever runs your site to check the `robots.txt` file allows it:

   ```
   User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
   Allow: /
   ```

   Also check that your hosting or security firewall isn't blocking it. OpenAI lists its crawlers in [its crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).
2. **Claim your business on Bing Places** Microsoft's Copilot answers from Bing, and ChatGPT tells owners to set up Bing Places alongside Google. It's free at [bingplaces.com](https://www.bingplaces.com/), and you can import your Google profile so the details match exactly.
3. **Claim your business in ChatGPT** OpenAI's Business Hub lets you claim your listing inside ChatGPT, correct your details, get a verified badge and see how often ChatGPT shows you. [Step-by-step: claim your business in ChatGPT](../guide/claim-your-business-in-chatgpt/index.html).

With those done, AI can find you. The eight steps below are what make it choose you.

## The 8 steps, in order

1. **See where you stand** Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google the questions your customers ask, with your city. Write down who is named and which sources each assistant cites. Those sources are your map. The [prompt finder](../tools/prompt-finder/index.html) gives you the questions; a [free check](../tools/ai-visibility-check/index.html) does it with you.
2. **Make your facts identical everywhere** Same name, address, phone and hours on your site, your Google profile and every directory. This is the foundation every other step depends on. [Directory listings guide](../signals/directory-listings/index.html).
3. **Complete and activate your Google profile** Right category, every service, weekly posts, real photos. Google's AI and Gemini read it directly; other assistants see it through search. [Google profile guide](../signals/google-profile/index.html).
4. **Build a steady flow of reviews** Ask every customer, on more than one site, and reply to every review. Reviews that name the service are what AI quotes back. [Reputation guide](../signals/reputation/index.html).
5. **Give each service its own clear page** Answer the question in the first two sentences, add an FAQ and structured data. AI can't recommend you for a service your site barely mentions. [Web management guide](../signals/web-management/index.html) and [SEO guide](../signals/seo/index.html).
6. **Get onto the lists and articles AI reads** The sources the assistants cited in step 1 are your outreach list. [Best-of guides](../signals/best-of-guides/index.html), [third-party articles](../signals/third-party-articles/index.html) and [PR](../signals/pr/index.html).
7. **Add video and labeled photos** Short videos that answer customer questions, and photos named and described for what they show. [Video](../signals/video/index.html) and [photo codifying](../signals/photo-codifying/index.html).
8. **Check every month** AI answers change. Repeat step 1 monthly, compare, and work on whatever the winning businesses have that you don't. [AI monitoring guide](../signals/ai-monitoring/index.html).

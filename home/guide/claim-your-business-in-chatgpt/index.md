On November 10, 2026, OpenAI opened Businesses in ChatGPT: a way for owners to claim their business inside ChatGPT, keep its details current and see how often ChatGPT shows it. Here's how to do it, step by step, and what it will and won't change.

## How to claim your business in ChatGPT

1. **Sign in and find your business** Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar (or go to [chatgpt.com/business-hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub)). Search for your business by name and pick the listing that matches. Local businesses can currently claim only an existing US listing.
2. **Verify that it's yours** Choose one of the methods offered: a code by text or call to your business phone, a code to your business email, a TXT record added to your website's DNS, or your Shopify store. If someone else manages your domain, ask them to add the record.
3. **Wait for approval** OpenAI reviews the claim and emails you its decision or a request for more information.
4. **Verify your identity with Persona** To publish changes, see insights or get the blue check, you complete identity verification with Persona. Have a government ID, business registration details and proof you can represent the business ready. Make sure the name and address match your official records exactly.
5. **Update your details** Add or correct your website, description, category, logo and cover photo. OpenAI reviews changes before they appear, and ChatGPT can still draw on other sources.
6. **Add your team or agency** The admin who claimed the business can add teammates and grant an agency access. Use each person's own ChatGPT account, and never forward a personal claim invitation.

## What claiming does

- You can correct the details ChatGPT shows about you.
- A blue check appears once your Persona verification is approved.
- You see insights for the last 28 days: mentions, website clicks and the topics where you come up. For a local business, a mention counts when ChatGPT shows a result for your location.

## What it doesn't do

OpenAI is clear that claiming "does not guarantee higher placement or sales," and that ChatGPT keeps making its own recommendations. Advertising doesn't change its answers either. ChatGPT still decides from what it reads about you across the web: your reviews, your listings, articles about you and your own site.

## Before you claim: five things to check

- **Your name, address and phone match everywhere.** If your website, Google profile and listings disagree, you're handing ChatGPT a conflict. [Why it matters](../../signals/directory-listings/index.html).
- **Your hours are written out as text** on your site, not only in an image.
- **Your site isn't blocking OpenAI's crawler.** OpenAI says sites must allow OAI-SearchBot to appear in ChatGPT search.
- **Your site has LocalBusiness schema.** [Free schema generator](../../tools/schema-generator/index.html).
- **The person claiming can verify.** Whoever controls the business phone, email or domain should do it, or be there when it's done.

## Frequently asked questions

How do I claim my business in ChatGPT?

Sign in to ChatGPT, open Business Hub, search for your business, choose the matching listing and verify by phone, business email, website domain or Shopify. After OpenAI approves the claim, complete identity verification with Persona to publish changes and see insights.

Is claiming my business in ChatGPT free?

Yes. You don't need an Ads account or an Enterprise agreement to claim an eligible business.

Will claiming make ChatGPT recommend my business?

Not by itself. OpenAI says claiming doesn't guarantee higher placement. ChatGPT still recommends based on what it reads about you across the web.

Can my agency manage my ChatGPT business profile?

Yes. The admin who claimed the business can add teammates and grant an agency access in Business Hub.

Can local businesses outside the US claim a listing?

For now, local stores and restaurants can claim only an existing US listing.

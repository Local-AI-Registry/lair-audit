Print this page or work through it with your team. Each section links to a full guide.

## Facts

- ☐ Pick one official name, address, phone and hours
- ☐ Update your website, Google profile, Apple Maps and Bing Places
- ☐ Fix or close every other listing that differs

- ☐ Correct primary category, all secondary categories
- ☐ Every service listed with a description
- ☐ Weekly posts and monthly new photos

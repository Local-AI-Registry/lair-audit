How to get your local business recommended by AI

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 13, 2026
Short answerAI assistants recommend the local businesses they can confirm from many trusted sources. Make your details identical everywhere, complete your Google profile, build steady reviews, give each service a clear page, get onto the lists and articles AI reads, and check what each assistant says every month.

How AI decides which local business to recommend

When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best dentist near me?" or asks Google's AI for a plumber who can come today, the assistant doesn't hold a ranked list of every business. It looks the question up, reads what it can find, and names the few businesses it is most confident about.

Confidence comes from agreement. If your website, your Google profile, three review sites, a local news story and a best-of list all describe the same business at the same address doing the same thing, the assistant can name you without worrying it's wrong. If your details conflict, your reviews are thin and nobody else mentions you, it plays it safe and names someone else.

So the job isn't to trick an algorithm. It's to make your business the easiest one in your area to confirm.

The 8 steps, in order

  1. See where you standAsk ChatGPT, Gemini and Google the questions your customers ask, with your city. Write down who is named and which sources each assistant cites. Those sources are your map. The prompt finder gives you the questions; a free check does it with you.
  2. Make your facts identical everywhereSame name, address, phone and hours on your site, your Google profile and every directory. This is the foundation every other step depends on. Directory listings guide.
  3. Complete and activate your Google profileRight category, every service, weekly posts, real photos. Google's AI and Gemini read it directly; other assistants see it through search. Google profile guide.
  4. Build a steady flow of reviewsAsk every customer, on more than one site, and reply to every review. Reviews that name the service are what AI quotes back. Reputation guide.
  5. Give each service its own clear pageAnswer the question in the first two sentences, add an FAQ and structured data. AI can't recommend you for a service your site barely mentions. Web management guide and SEO guide.
  6. Get onto the lists and articles AI readsThe sources the assistants cited in step 1 are your outreach list. Best-of guides, third-party articles and PR.
  7. Add video and labeled photosShort videos that answer customer questions, and photos named and described for what they show. Video and photo codifying.
  8. Check every monthAI answers change. Repeat step 1 monthly, compare, and work on whatever the winning businesses have that you don't. AI monitoring guide.

How it differs by assistant

The foundation is the same everywhere, but each assistant leans on different sources. Google's AI answers and Gemini lean on Google's data. Copilot leans on Bing. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude run their own web searches and cite what they read.

What doesn't work

  • Buying reviews or posting your own. Review sites remove them and can penalize the business.
  • Paid press releases on their own. Easy to buy, so they prove little.
  • Hundreds of near-identical city pages. Thin pages weaken your whole site.
  • Hidden text written "for AI". Assistants read what people read. Write for customers.
  • Expecting one fix to do it. AI looks for agreement across many sources. It takes all eleven signals working together.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT or Gemini?

Not inside their local answers today. Recommendations come from what the assistant can find and confirm about you across the web.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most sites can be fixed with clearer service pages and structured data. See web management.

Is this different from SEO?

It overlaps a lot, but AI weighs reviews, outside mentions and consistency more than rankings alone. See AI visibility vs SEO.

Which assistant matters most?

For most local businesses, Google's AI answers and ChatGPT reach the most people today. The work for one helps the others.

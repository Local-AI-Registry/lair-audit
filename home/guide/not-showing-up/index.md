# Why doesn't ChatGPT recommend my business?

> The seven most common reasons ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI don't recommend a local business, and how to find and fix yours.

Source: https://localairegistry.com/guide/not-showing-up/
Updated: 2026-11-15
Publisher: Local AI Registry (https://localairegistry.com)

Troubleshooting

By [Steve Lee](../../team/steve-lee/index.html), founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 15, 2026

**Short answer** Usually because the assistant can't confirm enough about you, or it can confirm more about a competitor. Conflicting details, thin reviews, unclear service pages and no outside mentions are the most common causes.

## The seven most common reasons

01

**Your details conflict across the web**  
If listings show different phones, addresses or names, AI can't be sure which business is real. [How to fix it](../../signals/directory-listings/index.html)

02

**Your reviews are thin or old**  
Fewer, older reviews than nearby competitors make you the riskier recommendation. [How to fix it](../../signals/reputation/index.html)

03

**Your site doesn't clearly say what you do**  
If a service has no page of its own, AI has nothing to quote for it. [How to fix it](../../signals/web-management/index.html)

04

**Nobody else talks about you**  
No articles, lists or press means no outside proof. [How to fix it](../../signals/third-party-articles/index.html)

05

**You're missing from the lists AI reads**  
Assistants often answer "best" questions from local best-of lists. [How to fix it](../../signals/best-of-guides/index.html)

06

**Your Google profile is incomplete**  
Google's AI and Gemini lean on it directly. [How to fix it](../../signals/google-profile/index.html)

07

**The question is too far from what you offer**  
AI matches questions to services. If you never mention a service, you won't be named for it. [How to fix it](../../signals/seo/index.html)

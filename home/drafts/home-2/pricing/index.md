## Straight answers.

### Pricing

How much does it cost?

One monthly price, quoted after your free audit. It depends on your market and what your business needs, so we don't publish one number that fits nobody.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The Local AI Blueprint is month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

What's included?

Everything AI checks: all 11 parts of the Local AI Blueprint, a free website if yours needs to be rebuilt for AI, your own team and a monthly report. There's no menu of add-ons.

### Your website

Is the website really free?

Yes. Many sites need to be rebuilt before AI can read them well, so we include it for every client who needs it. You own it.

What if I like my current site or web team?

Keep them. On a Custom plan we work alongside your team and put our effort where the gaps are.

### Custom plans

Do you work with other agencies?

Yes. If you keep your SEO or website team, we agree who owns what and shift our effort to everything else. One shared plan, one report.

Do you work with multiple locations?

Yes. Custom plans cover groups and multi-location businesses, with one plan and one report across every location.

Does the 90-day guarantee apply?

It's included on the Local AI Blueprint plan. On Custom plans it's available on request, since part of the work may sit with your other teams.

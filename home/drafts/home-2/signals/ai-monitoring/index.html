How do I know what AI says about my business?

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry
Updated November 16, 2026
Short answerAsk it, regularly and the same way each time. Run the questions your customers ask across ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI answers, Perplexity and others every month, and record whether you're named, how you're described and which sources each assistant cites.

Why monitoring matters

AI answers change. Assistants update their models, change their sources and refresh their data. A business that was recommended last month can disappear without warning. Monitoring is how you notice, and the sources each assistant cites tell you exactly where to work next.

What we do for you

Once a month we ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask, and we keep the answer each one gave.

A question is a category, not a phrase. You pick the searches you want to win, and we write a set of questions around each one: what it costs, whether it is worth doing, who does it near the town, how to choose a provider.

ChatGPT and Perplexity are asked in a real browser and we keep a screenshot. Gemini is checked directly, so we keep its words but no picture.

We read every answer for your business name and save it under the question that produced it. If a month's check didn't run, it stays blank instead of guessing.

What you get

  • A page of your questions with the current answer under each one.
  • The screenshot, for every answer a browser capture landed, so you can see the assistant said it rather than take our word for it.
  • Whether you were named, and who was named instead.
  • A month by month line for each question, so a change is visible rather than argued.

How it runs

  1. Your questions are chosen You pick the searches that matter. We rank your full set by what it is worth commercially, collapse near-identical phrasings, and take the top ten.
  2. Each one is anchored to your town A question is asked the way a person in your area would ask it, so the answer we record is the answer someone near you would get.
  3. The assistants are asked ChatGPT and Perplexity in a browser with a screenshot, Gemini checked directly. A question is asked once per calendar month, so nothing is bought twice.
  4. The answer is read and stored We record the answer text, the businesses named in it, and where you came in. If a check fails, we record it as a failed check, never as you not being named.

Why it moves the AI answerCapture is the measurement, not the work. It tells you which questions you already win, and it is the only honest way to know whether the rest of the work moved anything.

Your monthly report

Every thirty days you get a PDF of what was done on your account and what moved, counted from the day you bought rather than from the first of the month.

One document holding the period: the work ledger, your rankings, the AI answers we captured, your reviews, your directories and the publications that carried you.

Your period is your own. A customer who bought on the 9th is owed one on the 9th, and the pass checks every day rather than firing on the 1st.

A person reads it before you do. Three days before your date the draft goes to your assigned expert and to two people here, and that window is when a wrong sentence gets corrected or the report gets held.

  • A PDF on your own date, emailed to everyone you have listed.
  • The work ledger for the period, item by item.
  • The AI answers captured that month, with the screenshots.
  • Rankings, reviews, directories and press, in one place.

How to do it

  1. Write 10 to 20 questions real customers ask, like "best [service] near [neighborhood]" or "who does [service] in [city]".
  2. Ask each one in each assistant you care about, from a logged-out browser where possible.
  3. Record: named or not, the words used to describe you, and every source cited.
  4. Repeat monthly and compare.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I get different answers each time?

Assistants vary their answers, and results depend on location and account history. That's why monitoring uses a fixed set of questions and looks at the pattern over time.

Which assistants should I track?

At least ChatGPT, Google's AI answers and Gemini. Add Perplexity, Copilot and others as your customers use them.

Which assistants do you ask?

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. ChatGPT and Perplexity are asked in a browser and screenshotted. Gemini is checked directly, so that one is words only.

How often does it run?

Once a calendar month per question. Paying for a plan also fires a capture straight away, so your answers page has real captures on day one rather than at the end of the month.

What happens when an assistant will not answer?

The attempt is recorded as a failure with the reason. We do not fill the gap with a guess, and the question is asked again on the next pass.

When does it arrive?

On your own thirty day date, counted from the day you bought. Not on the first of the month.

Who else gets it?

Everyone on the business with the monthly report switch on, plus anyone you list as a report recipient. If that leaves nobody, it goes to you.

Does a person check it?

Yes. Three days before your date it goes to your assigned expert and two people here as a preview, and it can be corrected or held in that window.

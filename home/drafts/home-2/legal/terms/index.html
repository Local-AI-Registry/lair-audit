SECTION 09

Our 90-day guarantee

For clients on a Program that includes the guarantee, Local AI Registry guarantees that your business will be named by the tracked Assistants within 90 days of the Effective date, as defined in your Proposal. In short: named in AI in 90 days. Guaranteed.

What's covered. Your Proposal lists your Tracked questions, the Assistants we track and what counts as being named. Tracked questions must be real questions customers ask about your services in your area. They can't include your business name.

How it's measured. On day 1 we record what each tracked Assistant says for your Tracked questions. Because AI answers vary, we ask each question repeatedly and report how often you're named, using the method on our methodology page. Your monthly report includes the answers as screenshots. Our reporting is the record for this guarantee.

Your part. The guarantee depends on you giving us the access we need (for example, to your Google Business Profile and website), responding to requests and approvals within five business days, and not running conflicting work on the same profiles during the guarantee period. Delays on these may pause the 90-day period; we'll tell you in writing if that happens.

If we miss it. If your business isn't named within 90 days and you've met the conditions above, we keep providing the Services at no charge until it is named, unless your Proposal states a different remedy. Send your claim in writing within 14 business days after the 90-day period ends. Cancelling before the 90 days are up ends eligibility.

## Straight answers.

### Understanding AI search

What is AI search?

People now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Google's AI answers, Siri and others where to go, instead of scrolling a list of links. The assistant answers with a few names. AI search is about being one of them.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked. AI search gets your business named in the answer. Rankings still matter, because AI reads the pages Google ranks, but AI also checks your listings, reviews, photos and what other sites say about you.

Why does local matter so much?

Local questions are where AI answers show up most. Our research found that 76.4% of local business searches trigger a Google AI answer, versus 13.7% of all searches.

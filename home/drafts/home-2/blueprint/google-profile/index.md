Questions we get asked

## Straight answers.

### Google profile

How often should I post?

Weekly is a good rhythm. It keeps the profile current without becoming a burden.

Do posts go out without me seeing them?

Every post comes to your inbox first with the date it goes live. If you'd rather approve each one yourself, set posts to ask you first.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Edit it in the queue and the edited text is what publishes. Declining a draft doesn't cost you the slot.

What stops a post making a medical claim?

A scan runs on every draft. For health-related businesses, an outcome promise, a recovery time or a typical result blocks the draft outright.

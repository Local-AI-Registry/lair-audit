Questions we get asked

## Straight answers.

### Reviews

Should I reply to bad reviews?

Yes, calmly and briefly, without sharing private details. A thoughtful reply shows customers and AI that you take care of problems.

Do you reply to bad reviews for me?

Only if you approve one. A one or two star review is read by our team first, and any reply is held until it reaches you.

How fast is the alert on a low-star review?

Within hours, for a review less than a fortnight old. Older ones are flagged on your reviews page without an email.

Will you rewrite the replies already on my listing?

The thin ones, and only ones we didn't write. Five in your first week and five a week after, up to twenty in total.

Questions we get asked

## Straight answers.

### Photos

Do stock photos hurt?

They don't prove anything about your business. Use them sparingly and never as the main image of a service page.

Do you write captions about what's in the photo?

No. We name the service, the business and the place, all facts we hold.

Can I choose when a photo goes out?

Yes. Set a date on the photo. There's still one photo a day, so a date is a place in the queue.

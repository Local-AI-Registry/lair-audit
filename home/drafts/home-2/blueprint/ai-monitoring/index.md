Questions we get asked

## Straight answers.

### AI monitoring

Why do I get different answers each time?

Assistants vary their answers, and results depend on location and account history. That's why monitoring uses a fixed set of questions and looks at the pattern over time.

Which assistants do you ask?

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. ChatGPT and Perplexity are asked in a browser and screenshotted. Gemini is checked directly, so that one is words only.

How often does it run?

Once a calendar month per question. Signing up also fires a capture straight away, so your answers page has real captures on day one.

Does a person check it?

Yes. Three days before your date it goes to your assigned expert and two people here as a preview, and it can be corrected or held in that window.

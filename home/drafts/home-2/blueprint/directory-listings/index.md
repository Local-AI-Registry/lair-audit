## Straight answers.

### Directory listings

Do I need to be on every directory?

No. Being correct on the directories that matter for your industry beats being listed everywhere with mixed details.

How long until AI picks up the fix?

It varies by assistant and source. Some update within days, others take weeks. Checking monthly is the reliable way to know.

What if I'm already on most of them?

Then the order is small. The audit runs first for that reason.

Choosing an AI visibility service

## Four questions to ask any provider.

### Who does the work?

You, inside software, or a team doing it for you.

### What do they measure?

Rankings and clicks, or what each AI assistant actually says about you, with screenshots.

### What happens off your website?

Most AI citations come from other sites.

### What about contracts?

Ask about contract length and whether anything is guaranteed.

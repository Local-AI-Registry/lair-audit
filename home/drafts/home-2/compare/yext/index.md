# Local AI Registry vs Yext

Yext is an enterprise platform for managing business listings and brand data across the web. Its Scout product tracks how brands appear in AI search and local results. Where the two part ways

## Different tools for different jobs.

Yext is built for brands that have a team to run software. It keeps data consistent, which matters, but our research shows consistent data is the starting line: most citations in local AI answers come from news, reviews and lists that a listings platform doesn't create.

Synup is a listings and reputation management platform for local businesses and agencies, with tools for listings sync, reviews, social posting and white-label reporting. Local AI Registry is a done-for-you program that gets local businesses named in AI answers, built on a public, verified record. Here's an honest look at how they differ.

Where the two part ways

## Different tools for different jobs.

Synup keeps listings and reviews organized for whoever logs in. It doesn't do the press, outside articles, video and best-of placements that our research shows carry most of the weight in local AI answers.

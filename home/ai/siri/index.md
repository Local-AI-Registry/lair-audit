Siri answers local questions using Apple Maps information, and Apple Intelligence brings more assistant features to iPhone. Apple Business Connect is the free tool that lets you control how your business appears in Maps, Siri and other Apple apps.

## Do you track Siri and Apple Intelligence?

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Siri and Apple Intelligence isn't in the monthly check yet. The good news: the work that gets you named by one assistant carries over to the others, because they all read the same kinds of sources. A business that's consistent, reviewed and written about is easier for every assistant to recommend.

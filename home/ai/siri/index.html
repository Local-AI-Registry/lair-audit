Siri and Apple Intelligence

How do I get my business to show up in Siri and Apple Maps?

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 13, 2026
Short answerWhen someone asks Siri for a nearby business, Apple draws on Apple Maps data. Claim your free Apple Business Connect listing, keep it accurate and complete, and make sure your reviews and details match everywhere else.

How Siri and Apple Intelligence chooses local businesses

Siri answers local questions using Apple Maps information, and Apple Intelligence brings more assistant features to iPhone. Apple Business Connect is the free tool that lets you control how your business appears in Maps, Siri and other Apple apps.

What to fix first

These signals carry the most weight for Siri and Apple Intelligence, in order:

01
Directory listings
Same name, address, phone and hours everywhere.
02
Reputation
Reviews, ratings and replies across sites.
03
Photo codifying
Photos labeled so AI can read what they show.
04
Google profile
Complete, active and posting every week.
05
Web management
A fast site with the code AI reads.

Three things to do this week

  1. Claim your listing in Apple Business Connect and fill in every field.
  2. Add photos, a logo and showcase posts, the Apple equivalent of Google posts.
  3. Keep hours current, because iPhone users rely on Siri for "open now" questions.

**Short answer** Meta AI lives inside Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger and can search the web for local questions. A complete Facebook page, an active Instagram and the same web signals that help other assistants all count.

## How does Meta AI choose which local businesses to recommend?

Meta AI is built into the apps many of your customers already use. For current local information it can search the web. Your own Facebook and Instagram presence is part of what it can see inside Meta's apps.

## Do you track Meta AI?

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Meta AI isn't in the monthly check yet. The good news: the work that gets you named by one assistant carries over to the others, because they all read the same kinds of sources. A business that's consistent, reviewed and written about is easier for every assistant to recommend.

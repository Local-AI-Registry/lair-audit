**Short answer** Perplexity searches the web for every answer and shows its sources. Being on the review sites, local guides and articles it cites, with consistent details and a clear website, is how you get named.

## How does Perplexity choose which local businesses to recommend?

Perplexity is an answer engine that always searches before it answers and lists numbered sources. That makes it the most transparent assistant to study: ask your customers' questions and you can see every page it relied on.

New data from our research team

**85.6%**

In a census of every venue in two markets, 85.6% were never recommended by any of four assistants. Being named was associated with having a website, more reviews, listed prices and web mentions (Pitenin 2026, preprint, reviewed in our brief). ## What does the research say?

From [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our study of how Google's AI answers local business questions. Every number links to its chart.

[**4.1%** of business-site citations came from beyond Google's first page](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-citations-from-outside-google-page-one-by-source)[**~35%** of cited sources stayed the same when the same question was worded differently](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-answer-source-overlap-by-wording)

**The AI goes past page one for news, not for business websites.** Only 4.1% of business-site citations came from outside Google’s first page, versus 48.8% for AOL/Yahoo/MSN and 39.8% for TEGNA stations. A business site has to rank to be used.Figure 13, [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-citations-from-outside-google-page-one-by-source), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-citations-from-outside-google-page-one-by-source.svg) · [PNG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-citations-from-outside-google-page-one-by-source.png)

**Ask the same thing in different words and two-thirds of the sources change.** “Is Target open on Easter” and “Target Easter hours,” asked the same day, shared about 35% of cited sources on average. The verdict stayed the same; the sources rotated.Figure 20, [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-answer-source-overlap-by-wording), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-answer-source-overlap-by-wording.svg) · [PNG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/ai-answer-source-overlap-by-wording.png)

**Short answer** Copilot draws on Bing search, so an accurate Bing Places listing, consistent directory details, reviews and a website that ranks in Bing are the foundation.

## How does Microsoft Copilot choose which local businesses to recommend?

Copilot is Microsoft's assistant across Windows, Edge, Bing and Microsoft 365. Its web answers are grounded in Bing search results, which means your Bing presence matters more here than anywhere else.

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Microsoft Copilot isn't in the monthly check yet.

**Short answer** Alexa answers local questions from partner data and the web. Accurate listings on the major directories and review sites, especially the ones that feed voice assistants, give Alexa the details it needs.

## How does Alexa+ choose which local businesses to recommend?

Amazon's newer Alexa+ assistant handles more conversational requests, including finding and booking local services. It relies on partner data sources and listings rather than one public index, which makes consistent, accurate directory data the priority.

## Do you track Alexa+?

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Alexa+ isn't in the monthly check yet. The good news: the work that gets you named by one assistant carries over to the others, because they all read the same kinds of sources. A business that's consistent, reviewed and written about is easier for every assistant to recommend.

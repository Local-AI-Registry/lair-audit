Alexa+

How do I get my business recommended by Alexa?

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 15, 2026
Short answerAlexa answers local questions from partner data and the web. Accurate listings on the major directories and review sites, especially the ones that feed voice assistants, give Alexa the details it needs.

How does Alexa+ choose which local businesses to recommend?

Amazon's newer Alexa+ assistant handles more conversational requests, including finding and booking local services. It relies on partner data sources and listings rather than one public index, which makes consistent, accurate directory data the priority.

New data from our research team
73%

73% of the 128,400+ local businesses Local AI Registry has scored are named by zero AI assistants (our estimate from AI-visibility checks). Local AI Registry data

88%

Do you track Alexa+?

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Alexa+ isn't in the monthly check yet. The good news: the work that gets you named by one assistant carries over to the others, because they all read the same kinds of sources. A business that's consistent, reviewed and written about is easier for every assistant to recommend.

What can I do this week?

  1. Make sure your listings on the major review and directory sites are claimed and correct.
  2. Keep your phone number and hours identical everywhere; voice users can't see a page to double check.
  3. Ask an Alexa device your customers' questions and note what it says.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add my business to Alexa directly?

Not through a single public form today. Alexa relies on partner and directory data, so accurate listings are the route.

Do voice answers name more than one business?

Often only one or two, which makes being the best-documented option more important.

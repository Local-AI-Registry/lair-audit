**Short answer** When Claude searches the web for a local question, it reads and cites public sources: review sites, guides, news and business websites. Consistent details, strong reviews and independent coverage give it the confidence to name you.

## How does Claude choose which local businesses to recommend?

Claude, made by Anthropic, can search the web when a question needs current information, such as where to find a service nearby, and it cites the pages it used. It tends to favor sources that agree with each other and to be careful about recommending businesses it can't confirm.

In a census of every venue in two markets, 85.6% were never recommended by any of four assistants. Being named was associated with having a website, more reviews, listed prices and web mentions (Pitenin 2026, preprint, reviewed in our brief).

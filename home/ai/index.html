How each AI assistant picks local businesses

By Steve Lee, founder of Local AI Registry · Updated November 13, 2026
Short answerEach assistant checks a business against the sources it trusts before naming it. Google's assistants lean on Google's data, Copilot on Bing, and ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude on their own web searches. The shared foundation is consistent facts, strong reviews and outside mentions.

Every assistant finds local businesses a little differently. Some lean on Google's data, some on Bing, some on their own search. The work that gets you recommended is mostly shared, but the priorities change. Pick an assistant to see how it chooses and what to fix first.

**Short answer** Gemini can draw on Google Search and Google Maps, so the businesses it recommends usually have complete Google Business Profiles, strong recent reviews and websites that rank for their services.

## How does Gemini choose which local businesses to recommend?

Gemini is Google's assistant, built into Android phones, Google apps and the web. For local questions it can use Google's search results and Maps data, which makes your [Google profile](../../signals/google-profile/index.html) especially important.

## What does the research say?

From [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our study of how Google's AI answers local business questions. Every number links to its chart.

[**76.4%** of local business searches showed a Google AI answer, more than any other topic](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category)[**6.5%** of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions were the businesses' own websites](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap)

**How Google’s AI builds an answer about a local business.** For local questions, Google’s AI assembled answers mostly from news coverage (67.5% of citations), rarely from the business itself (6.5%), and usually ended by telling people to check with their local store.Illustration 2, [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#how-google-ai-builds-a-local-answer-illustration), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/how-google-ai-builds-a-local-answer-illustration.svg) · [PNG](../../research/local-answer-gap/images/how-google-ai-builds-a-local-answer-illustration.png)

**Who Google’s AI cites for local business questions vs everything else.** In local answers, Gannett newspapers supplied 28.3% of citations and appeared in 90.3% of answers.

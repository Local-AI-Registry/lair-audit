**Short answer** Google's AI answers are built on Google Search and Google's local data. A complete, active Google Business Profile, strong reviews, pages that rank for your services and clear structured data on your website are the foundation.

## How does Google AI Overviews and AI Mode choose which local businesses to recommend?

AI Overviews appear above regular Google results, and AI Mode is a full conversational search. For local questions both draw on Google's search index and its local business data, the same information behind Google Maps. Google publishes guidance that the same practices behind good search results apply to its AI features.

New data from our research team

**76.4%**

Local business searches triggered a Google AI Overview 76.4% of the time, compared with 13.7% of all searches. [The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html)

**67.5%**

News outlets supplied 67.5% of the citations in Google's AI answers about local businesses. [The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html)

**70 of 147**

Brand websites ranked on page one for 147 of 157 brand questions, but AI cited them in only 70. [The Local Answer Gap, LAIR-R-2026-001](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html)

## What did 7,583 Google AI answers show?

For [The Local Answer Gap](../../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our research team pulled thousands of Google AI answers to local questions and read every source each one cited. Here is what Google's AI did with local businesses:

| Finding | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Of local business searches showed a Google AI answer, more than any other topic | 76.4% |
| Of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions were the businesses' own websites | 6.5% |
| Of citations in local AI answers came from news coverage | 67.5% |
| Of local AI answers told people to check with their local store | 83.6% |
| Local AI answers gave a street address or phone number | 1 of 207 |
| Sources cited per local AI answer, versus 8.1 for other topics | 7.2 |
| Of local AI claims were supported by the pages the AI cited | 87.8% |
| Of business-site citations came from beyond Google's first page | 4.1% |
| More likely: local TV station sites cited in local answers vs other answers | 18x |

Put simply: Google's AI shows up on most local searches, it rarely quotes the business's own website, and it leans on news and other outside sources. It also tells people to check with the store far more often than it gives them an address. The business that wins is the one other sites describe clearly and consistently.

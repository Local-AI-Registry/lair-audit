**Short answer** Grok, xAI's assistant inside X, searches the web and posts on X. The same web signals that help other assistants apply, plus an active, accurate presence on X.

## How does Grok choose which local businesses to recommend?

Grok answers with information from the web and from public posts on X. For local businesses the web side matters most: reviews, listings, guides and your site. A claimed X account with your correct details is a low-effort addition.

## Do you track Grok?

Our monthly check covers ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini today, the assistants most people use to find a local business. Grok isn't in the monthly check yet. The good news: the work that gets you named by one assistant carries over to the others, because they all read the same kinds of sources. A business that's consistent, reviewed and written about is easier for every assistant to recommend.

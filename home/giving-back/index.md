We give back

We exist to help local businesses keep up. So a portion of what we earn goes to the people already doing that work on the ground, plus one cause close to our founder's family.

[*Small business***Kiva** 0% loans that help US small businesses start and grow.](https://www.kiva.org/)[*Small business***SCORE** Free mentoring for small business owners from experienced volunteers.](https://www.score.org/)[*Main streets***Main Street America** Reviving the downtowns and neighborhood main streets where local businesses live.](https://www.mainstreet.org/)[*Close to home***Children's Heart Foundation** Funds research on congenital heart defects. Our founder's daughter needed open-heart surgery around the time he chose to focus on local and stay close to home.](https://www.childrensheartfoundation.org/)

## Why these four

Kiva funds the loans, SCORE gives the advice and Main Street America rebuilds the streets local businesses live on. The Children's Heart Foundation is personal: our founder [Steve Lee](../team/steve-lee/index.html)'s daughter needed open-heart surgery around the time he stepped off the corporate track to focus on local and stay close to home.

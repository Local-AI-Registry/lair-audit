## What this guarantee is, and what it isn't

Nobody can buy a recommendation from ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI, and nobody can force one. ChatGPT itself tells business owners to be wary of companies that promise guaranteed recommendations. It's right to.

- **We don't pay AI companies or game their systems.** We do the work that makes your business easy for AI to confirm: your facts, your profiles, your reviews, your pages and the outside sources AI reads.
- **We never buy reviews or fake mentions.** Those get businesses removed, not recommended.
- **What we guarantee is our side of it.** A written list of questions and assistants, a day-1 screenshot, the same questions asked again every month, and free work until you're named.

Founder story

I spent 20 years inside the systems that decide what people find. Then I became the local business owner on the other side of them, and I didn’t like what I saw.

Chapter 1

## Building the systems

I built and sold my first company at 14. From there I spent two decades in digital: in Search at Google, at Microsoft on HoloLens, at Razorfish, Spotify, Chase and Kaplan. I was Head of Digital at Evolus through its 2018 Nasdaq IPO. I founded UX Laboratory, which was acquired. Along the way I’ve helped more than 5,000 local businesses get found.

Chapter 2

## Choosing home

The corporate track meant living on planes. Around the time I was deciding what came next, my daughter needed open-heart surgery. That settled it. I stepped off the corporate track, stopped the constant travel and chose to stay local: close to my family, and close to the small businesses that make a community worth living in.

It’s why part of what we earn goes to the [Children’s Heart Foundation](../giving-back/index.html).

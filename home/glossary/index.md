## AEO (answer engine optimization)

**Writing and structuring content so assistants and search features can lift a direct answer from it.**

AEO puts the answer first: a clear two-sentence response to the question, followed by detail, an FAQ and [schema markup](../glossary/index.html#schema-markup). It overlaps with [GEO](../glossary/index.html#geo); AEO is about how your pages are written, GEO is about the wider set of sources AI trusts.

## AI Overviews

**The AI-written summaries Google shows above some search results.**

AI Overviews summarize an answer and link to sources. For local questions they draw on Google's search index and local business data. Google's AI Mode is a separate, conversational search built on the same data. See [how to show up in Google's AI answers](../ai/google-ai-overviews/index.html).

Related: [AEO (answer engine optimization)](../glossary#aeo/index.html), [Entity](../glossary#entity/index.html)

## AI Score

**Local AI Registry's measure of how ready a business is to be recommended by AI, based on the 11 signals.**

The AI Score looks at all [11 signals](../signals/index.html) AI uses to decide which local business to recommend and summarizes them in one number, with the detail behind it. Our [free AI visibility check](../tools/ai-visibility-check/index.html) starts with it.

Related: [AI visibility](../glossary#ai-visibility/index.html), [AI share of voice](../glossary#share-of-voice/index.html), [Local AI Blueprint™](../glossary#local-ai-blueprint/index.html)

## AI SEO

**A broad term for search optimization that accounts for AI answers as well as traditional rankings.**

People use AI SEO to mean anything from using AI tools to write content to optimizing for AI assistants. On this site we mean the second: making sure AI assistants recommend your business. See [GEO](../glossary/index.html#geo) and [AEO](../glossary/index.html#aeo).

Related: [GEO (generative engine optimization)](../glossary#geo/index.html), [AEO (answer engine optimization)](../glossary#aeo/index.html)

## AI share of voice

**The share of relevant AI answers that name your business compared with competitors.**

If you ask 20 customer questions across several assistants and you are named in 6 of the answers, that is your share of voice for that question set. Tracking it monthly is the core of [AI monitoring](../signals/ai-monitoring/index.html).

Related: [AI Score](../glossary#ai-score/index.html), [AI visibility](../glossary#ai-visibility/index.html)

## AI visibility

**How often, and how well, AI assistants mention and recommend a business when people ask questions it could answer.**

AI visibility is to assistants what search rankings are to Google. A business with strong AI visibility is named when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI answers for the kind of service it offers, and it is described accurately. It depends on [eleven signals](../signals/index.html), from [consistent listings](../signals/directory-listings/index.html) to [reviews](../signals/reputation/index.html).

Related: [GEO (generative engine optimization)](../glossary#geo/index.html), [AEO (answer engine optimization)](../glossary#aeo/index.html), [AI Score](../glossary#ai-score/index.html)

## Citation

**A mention of your business on another website, or a source an AI assistant links to in its answer.**

The word has two uses. In local SEO, a citation is any listing or page that mentions your business details. In AI answers, a citation is a source the assistant shows to back up what it said. Being cited by AI usually starts with being cited around the web.

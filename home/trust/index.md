## A record is only worth the standards behind it.

We keep the public record of what AI says about local business. That only means something if the method is open, the rankings are never for sale, and you can see and control your own entry. Here is how we hold that line.

What we hold ourselves to

## The standards.

01

### The method is public

Eight signals, one number, every weight written down. You can read exactly how a score is built, line by line, on the methodology page.

02

### We never sell rank

No business can buy a higher score or a softer write-up, and no one can pay to be removed from a rival’s list. Independence is the whole point of a record.

03

### Your entry, your control

See what we hold about your business, correct anything wrong, or remove yourself from the register. The controls are below, and they’re free.

04

### We fix mistakes

Flag anything inaccurate and a real person reviews it, usually within two business days. A public record has to be a correct one.

05

### We don’t manufacture certainty

When the data is thin, we say so rather than inventing a number. A score you can’t trust is worse than no score at all.

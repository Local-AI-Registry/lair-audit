## What we hold, and why.

We keep a public record of how AI assistants describe local businesses. Here’s what that record holds, where it comes from, and what you can do about yours.

01

## What we record

For each business: the public facts, name, address, category, hours, website, public contact details, alongside what AI assistants say when asked to recommend a business like it, a summary of public reviews and ratings, and the AI Score we calculate from all of it.
02

## Where it comes from

Public sources only: the open web, business listings, mapping providers, and the answers we get by putting real questions to AI assistants. We don’t buy private data, and we don’t track individual people.

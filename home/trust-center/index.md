## The guardrails built into the work

Trust isn't a badge. It's what the system refuses to do. These rules run on every account:

- **No medical claims.** A scan runs on every draft and on the title. An outcome promise, a recovery time, a typical result or a safety word blocks the draft outright for health-adjacent categories.
- **No described before and after photos.** A photo classified as a result is never described and never published by us. Advertising rules on outcome claims apply to a picture the same way they apply to a sentence.
- **Bad reviews wait for you.** Only if you approve one. A one or two star review is read by our team first, and where a reply is written it is held until it reaches you, so nothing goes up under your name unless you approve it.
- **Press releases wait for your yes.** A press release never goes out unless you approve it. If the deadline passes without you sending it, we cancel the order, tell you we did not buy it, and draft a fresh one on the next round.
- **Nothing buys itself.** Queued, never auto-confirmed. A person on our team confirms every purchase, under a spending limit, so nothing buys itself.
- **A failed check is recorded as a failure.** We record the answer text, the businesses named in it, and where you came in. If a check fails, we record it as a failed check, never as you not being named.
- **A person reads your report first.** Yes. Three days before your date it goes to your assigned expert and two people here as a preview, and it can be corrected or held in that window.

WordPress: The platform behind a large share of local business websites. Here's how Local AI Registry works with it to get you named in AI answers.

Why WordPress matters for AI

Your own website is only a small share of what AI cites, and much of what AI attributes to official sites isn't actually supported by them. The fix is making your site easy for AI to read and verify.

51.6%

For claims AI attributed to a store locator, app or official site, 51.6% were not supported by any page it cited.

Source: The Local Answer Gap · LAIR-R-2026-001

What we do with WordPress

  • Schema markup that states your facts in code
  • An llms.txt file and AI-friendly robots rules
  • Speed and technical fixes
  • Articles that answer the questions your customers ask AI
  • We work with the site you have whenever we can

The Local AI Blueprint™

Short answerOne plan, one team and one monthly report that works all 11 signals AI checks, so assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI recommend your business. Backed by our 90-day guarantee.

What it is

The Local AI Blueprint™ is how Local AI Registry gets a local business recommended by AI. Instead of buying SEO, listings, PR, video and reviews from different vendors, you get one plan that works all eleven signals together, one team accountable for it, and one report.

Why it starts with one verified record

Every piece of work comes from the same verified facts: your name, address, hours, services, prices you're happy to share, and the people who do the work. Your Google posts, directory listings, website, press and review replies all say the same thing because they come from the same place. That agreement is what makes AI confident enough to name you.

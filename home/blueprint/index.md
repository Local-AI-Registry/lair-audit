## What does the research say?

From [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html), our study of how Google's AI answers local business questions. Every number links to its chart.

[**6.5%** of the sources Google's AI cited for local questions were the businesses' own websites](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#who-google-ai-cites-for-local-business-questions-heatmap)[**~35%** of cited sources stayed the same when the same question was worded differently](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#ai-answer-source-overlap-by-wording)[**76.4%** of local business searches showed a Google AI answer, more than any other topic](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#google-ai-overview-activation-by-search-category)

**One confirmed record, pushed everywhere AI looks.** Because AI rotates between sources, a business’s facts need to match on its website, Google Business Profile, directories and press. Local AI Registry starts from one owner-confirmed record.Illustration 6, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#single-source-of-truth-for-ai-local-aeo-illustration), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/single-source-of-truth-for-ai-local-aeo-illustration.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/single-source-of-truth-for-ai-local-aeo-illustration.png)

**Local AEO priority map: what AI cites vs what you control.** The sources AI cites most for local answers (news) are the ones businesses control least, and the source they fully control (their site) is cited least. AEO closes that gap from both sides.Figure 23, [The Local Answer Gap](../research/local-answer-gap/index.html#local-aeo-priority-2x2-control-vs-citation-share), Local AI Registry Research, 2026 · [SVG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/local-aeo-priority-2x2-control-vs-citation-share.svg) · [PNG](../research/local-answer-gap/images/local-aeo-priority-2x2-control-vs-citation-share.png)

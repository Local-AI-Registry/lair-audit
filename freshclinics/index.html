How AI decides who to recommend

More clinic owners now ask ChatGPT instead of scrolling Google. AI doesn't show ten links. It writes one answer and names a few companies. Here's how it picks them.

It starts with a clear, unique story

AI recommends companies it can sum up in one sentence. Before any of the 11 checks, a company needs one simple line about what it is and why it's different, said the same way everywhere.

Then AI checks 11 places. We call this Your Blueprint™

1Directory listings

On the comparison lists and directories AI reads.

2PR

Recent news about the company.

3Video

Videos people actually watch, including customer stories.

4SEO

Ranking on Google for what buyers search.

5Web management

A website AI can read: clear facts, sitemap, fact sheet.

6Reputation

Real reviews on sites the company doesn't own.

7Google profile

A complete Google Business Profile with reviews.

8Photo codifying

Images labeled so AI knows what they show.

9Independent articles

Other people writing about the company.

10Independent guides

How-to guides that rank and get quoted.

11AI monitoring

Asking AI every month and tracking who it names.

Why this is happening

The story first, then the 11 checks

The foundation: no single, clear story for Fresh

Spakinect has one sentence, and ChatGPT repeats it almost word for word. Fresh describes itself differently almost everywhere it appears, so AI has no simple story to retell.

WhereHow Fresh is described
Fresh homepage title"Cosmetic & Aesthetic Clinic Software"
Fresh on job sites"A medical technology company on a mission to elevate care across the aesthetics and proactive healthcare space"
Fresh on LinkedIn"The leading clinical infrastructure for independent aesthetic practices"
Spakinect acquisition press release"The world's leading network of independent Med Spas operating within the same medical infrastructure"
How ChatGPT labeled Fresh"Medical aesthetics membership and clinical support platform"
WhereHow Spakinect is described
Spakinect homepageA virtual Good Faith Exam provider trusted by 4,500+ med spas, backed by W-2 practitioners
What ChatGPT told a buyerMemberships from $99 a month, W-2 employed clinician team, 1.56 million completed exams, average 31‑second connection
Software, technology company, clinical infrastructure, network or membership? Each is true, but AI can't recommend all five. Fresh needs one sentence that says what it is and why it's different from Moxie, Portrait and the medical director companies, then needs to repeat that sentence everywhere.

What we'd do

A 90-day plan for both brands

Story first, then the 11 checks, starting with the ones holding Fresh back the most.

  1. Pick one story (week 1)Agree on one sentence that says what Fresh is and why it's different, and a matching one for Spakinect that makes clear how the two fit together. Every step below repeats it.
  2. Fix the website and Google profile (weeks 1 to 2) · checks 5, 7 and 8Add the fact sheet and AI guide file to Fresh's site, rewrite the homepage for US med spa owners, describe the images, and fill in the US Google profile. Make the exam price and state list match everywhere.
  3. Make the two brands agree (weeks 1 to 2) · checks 5 and 9Update Spakinect's ranking pages so they stop scoring Fresh as an unknown, and tell the Fresh and Spakinect story on both sites.
  4. Get listed (weeks 2 to 8) · checks 1 and 9Get Fresh onto the comparison lists AI quotes for medical directors, starting a med spa and practice support, with accurate details.
  5. Build the guides buyers search for (weeks 3 to 10) · checks 4 and 10Combine the three "start a med spa" articles into one strong guide. Add a medical director page for each state Fresh serves, the same way Spakinect's state exam pages already rank #1.
  6. Get talked about (ongoing) · checks 2, 3 and 6Ask US members for reviews on independent sites, turn member stories into short videos like Spakinect's, and earn mentions in industry news. This answers the exact doubt ChatGPT raised.
  7. Check the scoreboard monthly · check 11Re-ask the same buyer questions every month and track who AI names, so you can see the needle move.

Other website checks

  • FreshSitemap is in placefreshclinics.com/sitemap.xml is live and lists 460 pages. The robots.txt file is empty, which is harmless, though adding a line that points to the sitemap is good practice.
  • FreshThree main headings on the homepageThe homepage has three top-level headings, which blurs what the page is about.
  • SpakinectSelf-written "best of" rankingsSeveral Spakinect articles rank Spakinect first. Perplexity warned users that some rankings come from vendors selling competing services, which hurts trust.
  • SpakinectReview stars added to its own homepage codeThe homepage marks up its own star rating. Google doesn't show stars a business adds about itself, so this won't earn stars and can look like an attempt to game results.
  • SpakinectWeak homepage basicsThe main heading is just "Med Spas," and the page description ends with a stray character.
  • SpakinectTraffic sliding since winterEstimated US visits fell from about 912 a month in February 2026 to about 736 in October.

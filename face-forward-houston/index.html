Your Houston competitors are already being namedThe same few names come up again and again.

We asked ChatGPT and Perplexity, from Houston, who to see for a deep plane facelift and neck lift. Between them they named Dr. Michel Siegel, Dr. Chaowen Wu, Dr. Sean Delaney, Dr. Henry Mentz, Dr. Bob Basu, Dr. Raghu Athré, Dr. Haresh Yalamanchili and Dr. Russell Kridel. Neither named you.

One detail stands out. Perplexity read Dr. Basu’s llms.txt, a short file written for AI that sums up a practice, and used it in its answer. Your site doesn’t have one yet.

On Google’s map, Dr. Roth Plastic Surgery (4.9 stars, 1,300 reviews) and Dr. Bradford Patt (4.8, 318) hold the top spots for facelift, rhinoplasty and “facial plastic surgeon houston.” Your rating matches theirs. What’s missing is the signal around it.

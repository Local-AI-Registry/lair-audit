3What AI reads about PCVI is thin, and some of it is wrongBlog titles listed as services, no doctors named, 0 of 9 patient questions answered.

AI builds its answer from the sources that describe you. Here’s what those sources say today.

  • Services: 16 are pulled from blog post titles: “Contact thank,” “Retina marches beat drum,” “January glaucoma awareness month.” Cataract surgery, your best-reviewed procedure, appears only as an AI guess.
  • Doctors: none listed. AI can’t say who performs your surgery or why their training matters.
  • Patient questions: 9 asked on your profile (“Booking a consult, what happens?” “Aftercare, what does it involve?”), 0 answered.
  • Directories: listed on 3 of the 33 we checked (Yelp, BBB, Healthgrades). Missing from Apple Maps, Zocdoc, WebMD and Vitals.
  • Address: Perplexity gives 360 S. Garden Way and drops Suite 250.
  • Reviews: AI reads your reviews as excellent cataract outcomes and thorough consults, with complaints about wait times and follow-up on contact lens orders.

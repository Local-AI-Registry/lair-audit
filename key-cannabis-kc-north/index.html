The biggest fix

AI still thinks you’re partly Elevate.

AI only recommends a business when the same facts show up everywhere. The rename from Elevate to Key left two versions of KC North online, and AI is splitting the difference. These are all quick to fix.

Leafly

Page address is still elevate-kc-north, and the phone is (816) 648-6402, not (816) 831-3871.

Weedmaps

Search results still show “Elevate Missouri - KC North”, and that link now opens a general Kansas City list. No live Key KC North page found.

keycannabis.com

The /kc-north/ page lists (802) 831-3871 and its click-to-call uses it. Perplexity has already picked up that number.

How AI decides

One question becomes six searches

Plain English

When a patient asks ChatGPT about a non-surgical nose job, it quietly looks up cost, safety, candidacy and more before it answers. If your site has a strong page for each of those, you show up in every lookup.

Under the hood

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) engines run query fan-out. The 11-tier topical map mirrors the sub-queries, and each money page only publishes once its prerequisite chain is live, so every branch resolves inside one interlinked cluster.

The moat

Entity-semantic RAG: why a $10 tool cannot do this

Listing tools push the same name, address and phone to the same directories for every business. Every competitor already has that. What gets a physician named is a corroborated entity sitting inside the meaning-space an engine searches, and that is built from assets software cannot produce.

An entity graph, not a listing

Plain English

AI checks whether the same facts about you appear in many trusted places: journals, trial records, news, video. A listings tool only repeats your address. The program builds every one of those connections.

Under the hood

Entity resolution keys on corroborated triples (Rivkin, authored, PMMA rhinoplasty study; Rivkin, PI, Volux trial). The sameAs graph, research hub, syndication and press each add an independent edge. NAP consistency is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.

Semantic coverage, measured

Plain English

Every question patients ask sits somewhere on a map of meaning. Each month we write into the empty spots where nobody, including you, has a good answer yet.

Under the hood

The 300-question panel is embedded and clustered. Every published passage is embedded too, and nearest-neighbor distance shows which questions have no Rivkin passage within retrieval range. That gap list sets the next month's articles and videos.

Source agreement

Plain English

When your website, Google profile, RealSelf and the news all say the same thing about you, AI trusts it and repeats it.

Under the hood

Rerankers weight agreement across unrelated domains. The approved credential sentence is used verbatim on every surface, and syndication plus milestone press supply the independent domains a single site cannot.

First-source advantage

Plain English

We publish on new techniques days after they are presented at conferences, weeks before anyone else writes about them. Later articles end up pointing back to you.

Under the hood

With no competing passages in the index, retrieval has one candidate. Fresh, primary-sourced white papers also earn the inbound citations that compound authority after the topic gets crowded.

Bylined medical articles that go further

  • About 1,800 words each, under Dr. Rivkin's byline or a rotating, independent medical director's reviewed byline

Original research white papers

  • A podium talk from AAFPRS, ASDS, AMWC or Vegas Cosmetic Surgery, turned into a white paper
  • Combined with AI-powered synthesis of the newest trials, FDA filings and preprints
  • Published with methodology and ScholarlyArticle schema before consumer content exists

Creates facts no other source holds yet, so synthesis has to credit the practice, and feeds the month's syndicated story.

1 a month

National syndication

  • One data-led story a month through a national newswire content network
  • Republished across hundreds of news sites, built on that month's white paper

Hundreds of independent domains stating the same facts is the corroboration rerankers and training corpora weight most.

4+ a month, plus same day

Motion explainer videos

  • Illustrated motion explainers with professional voiceover, one per priority treatment
  • When a treatment term spikes on Google Trends, an explainer is made and auto-published to YouTube that day
  • Captions, chapters and transcripts, embedded on the matching page

YouTube is among the most cited domains in AI answers on treatments. Transcripts and VideoObject schema make every video retrievable as text.

1 a week

Community presence

  • One helpful contribution a week in the threads patients use to research these treatments
  • Posted from a disclosed practice account
  • A monthly map of the threads AI engines cite for the 300 monitored questions

Reddit is a top-cited source in AI answers. Disclosed participation keeps the threads standing; undisclosed promotion gets removed.

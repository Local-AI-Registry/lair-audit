Gap

"Best" answers come from proof CES does not have in Louisville

AI built its "best" lists from things CES lacks in Louisville: surgeon credential pages on third-party sites (Healthgrades, UofL faculty pages, DuPont Surgery Center's own surgeon profile for Dr. Cassol), large review counts, and dedicated LASIK and cataract pages for the city. Our surgeons' outside profiles point to Lexington, Indiana or a competitor (section 4).

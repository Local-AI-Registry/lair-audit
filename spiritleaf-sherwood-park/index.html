The biggest fix

AI is blending your two stores into one.

AI only recommends a business with confidence when the same facts show up everywhere. Right now Brentwood’s facts disagree with each other, and some of Salisbury’s have leaked in. A shopper sent to the wrong address or told you close at 6 on a Sunday goes to one of the 15 or so other stores in town instead.

ChatGPT

Gave Brentwood’s address as 115 Tisbury Street. That’s your Salisbury store. It also gave closing times of 6 PM and midnight; you close at 10.

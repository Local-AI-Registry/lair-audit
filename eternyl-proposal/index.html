What AI says when someone asks about you by name

We asked ChatGPT, "What is Eternyl Beauty in NYC and is it good?" The good news: it knows you, and it would put you on a shortlist. But four things in its answer cost you patients:

  • It doubts your reviews. It cites an independent med spa review site that rated you 3 out of 5 for "limited recent review activity," and tells people not to rely on your five stars alone.
  • It puts you in Midtown. So you're left out of Upper East Side questions, where demand and prices are higher.
  • It guesses at your menu. It lists nonsurgical nose jobs and body contouring, and barely mentions medical weight loss, one of your two focus areas.
  • It tells patients to check your licenses. Your site doesn't show your RN credentials or what your medical director does, so AI can't vouch for you.

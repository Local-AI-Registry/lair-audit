3Your practice is described differently everywhere AI looksDifferent names, different hours, different years. AI can’t tell they’re all you.

AI trusts a business when every source agrees. Here’s what the sources say today:

  • Name: “Laser and Cosmetic Center” on Google, “Laser & Cosmetic Center” on your site, “Kipervas, Yelena, DO” on Superpages with no mention of the center at all.
  • Hours: your website offers Saturdays by appointment. Google says Saturday closed. A local asked on your profile whether you’re open Saturdays, and nobody has answered.
  • Experience: 24 years on your site, 25 on RealSelf.
  • RealSelf, the site patients and AI use most for cosmetic providers: your physician profile has no practice name, no website, no photo and 0 reviews. It ranks #3 on RealSelf’s Stroudsburg Botox page, so people are seeing it.
  • Superpages: no hours, and the website link points to your providers page instead of your home page.
  • Google description begins with stray formatting characters (“*|Laser & Cosmetic Center|*”), which AI copies word for word.
  • Team: your site lists three providers, including Lesia for permanent makeup. Your Registry record lists two.
  • Directories: not on Birdeye’s or PortraitCare’s Stroudsburg med spa pages. Your Registry record lists no directories at all.

One more reason consistency matters: RealSelf also lists a practice called The Center for Laser and Aesthetic Medicine in Stroudsburg. With names that close, AI needs your facts to match everywhere to keep the two apart.

Sources: Local AI Registry record, RealSelf Stroudsburg Botox listings, Superpages, Birdeye and PortraitCare, all read November 13, 2026.

5Your website undersells you to Google and AIPlaceholder descriptions, thin service pages, and a “Text Us” button that can’t text.

Your site looks professional to a visitor. What Google and AI read underneath is thinner:

  • Search descriptions are placeholders. The text Google shows under your pages reads “Specials -”, “Our Providers -” and “Dermal Fillers in Stroudsburg -”.
  • Page titles repeat themselves, for example “Dermal Fillers in Stroudsburg Stroudsburg Cosmetic and Laser Specialist.”
  • Service pages are short and dated. Dermal fillers runs about 600 words, last updated May 2025, with no prices, no FAQs and no before-and-afters. Hyperhidrosis is about 130 words and isn’t in the menu. PRF hair restoration has no page; its link goes to the general services page.
  • No structured data found on the service pages we checked, so Google and AI can’t read who performs each treatment or that you’re a physician-led practice.
  • The home page is slow. Google’s own speed test scores it 41 out of 100. The main image takes 4.8 seconds to appear on a fast desktop connection, and the page weighs nearly 12 MB.
  • Booking is a request form, not live times, and it doesn’t say when the office will reply.
  • The “Text Us” button on every page is set to your Gmail address instead of a phone number, so it won’t open a text on a patient’s phone.
  • Small things AI notices: “Lase and Cosmetic Center” in Sandra’s bio, “Cherry FInancing” site-wide, and “60+” testimonials when Google shows 83.

Your site is built and hosted by Doctor Multimedia. We’d coordinate with them, or hand them a punch list, whichever you prefer.

Source: drkipervas.net home, providers, appointments, specials, dermal fillers and hyperhidrosis pages, read November 13, 2026; Google Lighthouse test via DataForSEO, November 13, 2026.

What patients praise: natural-looking Botox, filler and thread results, laser hair removal, hydrodermabrasion and carbon peel facials, and Plexr for age spots.

What a few flagged: mixed messages about treatment plans and slow follow-up after procedures. Both are fixable with a simple post-visit text, and worth fixing before more people read them.

The risk: the “Rate Us” stars on every page send some star choices to Google and the others to a private “What can we improve” form. Google’s review policy doesn’t allow steering only happy patients to Google, and it can lead to reviews being removed. The fix is simple: one review link for everyone, plus a separate “tell Dr. Kipervas directly” option that anyone can use.

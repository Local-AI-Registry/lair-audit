How AI decides who to name

AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It’s SEO for AI. SEO gets you onto Google’s list of links. AEO gets your name into the answer itself.

Old way: SEO and ads

New way: AI answers

Google still matters: most people double-check an AI pick on Google or Maps before they call. That’s why we do both, SEO and AEO, as one job.

What happens inside the AI

A patient asks. AI looks you up in dozens of places. Then it writes an answer from what it found. When those places agree, it names you.

1. A patient asks

2. AI looks it up

3. AI writes the answer

“But I already show up in AI”

You might, sometimes. AI visibility isn’t yes or no. It’s a percentage, like a weather forecast.

Ask ChatGPT the same question ten times, or have ten different people ask it, and you’ll get different answers. Wording, location, and which AI they use all change the result. So the real question isn’t “do I show up?” It’s “how often?”

40%

Means AI names you in 4 of every 10 answers. The other 6 go to someone else.

80%+

Is what we work toward: named in most answers, across ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity.

We measure this for you every month, across many versions of the questions patients actually ask, so you see the real percentage instead of one lucky (or unlucky) search. We already measured yours: when ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity were asked for recommendations, Mill Valley Dermatology was named in 16 of 32 answers. That’s 50%, and an AI Score of 47, third among Mill Valley dermatologists behind Aesthetic Dermatology & Skin Cancer (52) and Zand Dermatology (48).

What we did

The work behind the numbers.

Most of the early work was foundation: fixing what was quietly holding the site back, so every page after it ranks faster. Your team shipped a lot of it from our audits, and it shows in the results above.

Two full site audits and a growth roadmap

Delivered

A technical audit, a content audit, a topical map of everything Atrium should rank for, a page by page URL map and a phased content roadmap. Followed by a shared project tracker so both teams see what is open.

Valley Village menu fixed

Shipped with your team

We found a redirect loop that kept Google out of the Valley Village menu, more than 2,000 product pages. It was fixed in August. A fresh crawl confirmed every menu page now loads and sits in the sitemap.

Site code errors cut from 9,210 pages to 5

Shipped with your team

The structured data that tells Google and AI what Atrium is had errors on 9,210 pages. After the fix we recommended, a recrawl found errors on 5.

28 city pages rebuilt

Shipped with your team

Every city page got its own title, heading, local copy and FAQ markup. This is the template behind the West Hills, Calabasas, Malibu and Agoura Hills gains above.

Duplicate pages cleaned up

Shipped with your team

Five overlapping Valley Village posts merged into the location page. Delivery and online ordering pages merged into one hub. 25 duplicate brand pages reduced to one each. 19 cultivation articles folded into 14 stronger guides. Pages stop competing with each other, so the right one ranks.

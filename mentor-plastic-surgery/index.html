Your Google listing name breaks Google's rules

It's the one asset you can't afford to lose, and today it's at risk.

  • Business name on GoogleGoogle allows the real-world name only, no keywordsKeyword-stuffed
  • City in the name"Cleveland," for an office in ConcordMismatch

  • Beachwood locationLegacy Surgery Center, where you offer AYONNot on Google

The detail

Google's guidelines say the listing name must match the real-world name on your sign and website: "Mentor Plastic Surgery & MedSpa." Adding "Plastic Surgeon Cleveland" can get the listing suspended without warning, and a competitor can report it. Suspension takes your 91 reviews and your map spot offline until Google reinstates it.

The fix is quick and safe in the right order: rename, clean the service list, then handle the old listing so its 3.5 stars stop showing next to your name.

Source: DataForSEO Google business listing data for Concord, OH (last updated Sept 19, 2026) and Google's guidelines for representing your business, Nov 18, 2026.

It is the one asset you can't afford to lose, and today it's at risk.

  • Business name on GoogleGoogle allows the real-world name only, no keywordsKeyword-stuffed
  • City in the name"Cleveland," for an office in ConcordMismatch
  • Service descriptionsDozens of services end in the same 5 hashtagsLooks like spam
  • Second listing"Vanek Plastic Surgery," 3.5 stars, 29 reviewsClean up
  • Beachwood locationLegacy Surgery Center, where you offer AYONNot on Google
  • Last listing update we can seeSeptember 19, 20262 months
The detail

Google's guidelines say the listing name must match the real-world name on your sign and website, "Mentor Plastic Surgery & MedSpa." Adding "Plastic Surgeon Cleveland" can get the listing suspended without warning, and a competitor can report it. Suspension takes your 91 reviews and your map spot offline until Google reinstates it.

The fix is quick and safe when done in the right order: rename, clean the service list, then handle the old listing so its 3.5 stars stop showing next to your name.

Source: DataForSEO Google business listing data for Concord, OH (listing last updated Sept 19, 2026) and Google's guidelines for representing your business, Nov 18, 2026.
